# DEC-0001 Proposal — Foundational Social-Fabric Model

**Status:** PROPOSED — not accepted  
**Authority level:** Constitutional  
**Date opened:** 2026-09-19  
**Approver required:** Human Project Director

## Problem

RES-0001 strongly supports keeping kinship, affiliation, support, residence, property claims, labor/service, dependency, institutional entitlements, documentary association, and physical space analytically distinct. The project now needs a concrete causal foundation capable of representing those distinctions without reducing households to fixed containers or duplicating the simulation at a higher level.

## Proposed foundational model

The simulation's foundational social reality should consist of persistent **individuals**, **material resources and places**, **historically meaningful relationships / claims / obligations**, and **events/processes that create, modify, fulfill, contest, transmit, or terminate them**.

A **household** should be a persistent collective entity that **emerges from and remains grounded in** sufficiently durable configurations of those lower-level realities rather than serving as the primitive container from which residence, kinship, property, labor, support, or status are inferred.

Once a household exists, it may possess persistent identity, history, coordinated resources or practices, and causal effects on individuals. Its continuity may outlive particular members. However, consequential household state and action must remain historically legible through the people, relationships, resources, norms/rules, places, and prior events that sustain it.

No single relationship should silently imply the others. In particular, household affiliation must not automatically imply co-residence, kinship, ownership, labor, support, dependency, or exclusive attachment.

The same causal pattern should be capable of extending upward later: institutions may become persistent actors while remaining grounded in the lower-level social reality through which they operate.

## Why this is being proposed now

The Seed already establishes individuals and relationships as foundational, households as the first major collective structure, recursive causality, emergence, and causal genealogy. RES-0001 makes the consequences more concrete: the historical evidence repeatedly warns against collapsing distinct social, legal, economic, spatial, and institutional relations into one household-membership variable.

## What this proposal does not decide

It does **not** choose:
- a programming language, engine, database, ECS, graph library, or storage model;
- an event-sourced, state-based, graph-based, object-oriented, relational, or other software architecture;
- an algorithm or threshold for household recognition;
- exact historical frequencies or demographic parameters;
- a universal Old Babylonian or Mesopotamian household definition;
- individual decision-making algorithms;
- simulation timestep/execution strategy;
- performance/abstraction strategy;
- how temples, palaces, cities, or states are represented in code;
- a player role or conventional game loop.

## Evidence basis

The proposal is an evidence-informed simulation design synthesis from RES-0001 A–H. Those returns remain advisory pending formal historical-proposition reconciliation. Their strongest cross-cutting result is the need to distinguish relationships and histories rather than infer a total household from any single document, building, kin label, resource claim, or institutional connection.

Historical evidence is not being promoted directly into mechanics by this proposal. The proposal selects a causal *shape* intended to remain capable of representing the documented variety.

## Principal consequences if accepted

1. **Household is emergent but may become persistent.** It is neither a mere transient graph cluster nor a primitive container.
2. **Relationships and claims become first-class conceptual structure.** Their exact technical representation remains open.
3. **State changes require histories.** Formation, transfer, fulfillment, default, succession, dispute, status change, and termination become conceptually important.
4. **Resource objects and claims over them are separable.** Ownership, use, access, residence, service, and benefit cannot be assumed equivalent.
5. **Social position is multidimensional.** One scalar status hierarchy cannot be assumed to encode kinship, legal dependency, labor, property, institutional affiliation, or residence.
6. **Higher-order agency must be grounded.** Later institutions can persist and act, but should not become a disconnected macro-simulation.
7. **Observability is structural.** The simulation must ultimately explain why a collective exists, changes, acts, divides, persists, or dissolves.

## Reversibility

Conceptually expensive to reverse after substantial implementation. This is why approval is required before technical architecture research is allowed to optimize around it.
