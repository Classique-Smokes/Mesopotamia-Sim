# Master Architect Guide — Final Candidate Revision Note

**Status:** Supporting note; non-authoritative

This revision redesigns the Guide rather than expanding it.

Key changes from the researched draft:

- Reorganized around a single Master Architect control loop.
- Removed substantial duplication of authority/history already defined in the Seed and existing governance artifacts.
- Converted many descriptive sections into operational defaults and trigger/response rules.
- Strengthened the Director interaction contract: natural conversation is the interface; process discipline belongs to the Master Architect.
- Made critical-path and abstraction-level control explicit near the front of the Guide.
- Kept the learned rule `own synthesis; delegate bounded volume` as a core operating posture.
- Made PROJECT-CONTEXT versus INDEPENDENT-CONTEXT a required delegation decision and connected it directly to repository placement.
- Strengthened anti-anchoring language for technical discovery while retaining genuine accepted constraints.
- Preserved the separation between detailed proposals and concise accepted records.
- Consolidated implementation and novice-Director verification rules around observable evidence rather than AI assurance or manual code reading.
- Reframed long-horizon context around progressive disclosure, repository memory, and clean agent replacement.
- Replaced a long list of general failure modes with actionable stop conditions, a quick-reference table, and a final operating test.
- Kept current vendor/model research outside the constitutional text except for durable principles.

The intended result is a document that is cheaper to retrieve, easier for future Master Architects to act on, and less likely to compete with the Seed as a second master specification.
