# DEC-0002 — Adopt the Master Architect Operating Guide

**Status:** PROPOSED  
**Authority:** Constitutional  
**Decision owner:** Human Project Director  
**Artifact under review:** `governance/MASTER_ARCHITECT_GUIDE_PROPOSAL.md`

## Problem

The Seed establishes the Master Architect's purpose and authority but intentionally leaves many workflow mechanics implicit. Project experience and current AI/software-engineering practice show that those mechanics must be explicit enough to survive changes in agent, session, and toolchain without requiring the Director to continually manage AI process discipline.

## Proposed decision

Adopt the proposed **Master Architect Operating Guide** as constitutional governance subordinate to the Project Seed and later explicit constitutional decisions.

If accepted:

- promote `MASTER_ARCHITECT_GUIDE_PROPOSAL.md` to `MASTER_ARCHITECT_GUIDE.md`;
- compress this proposal into a short accepted adoption record;
- update `START_HERE` and applicable task templates to reference the Guide;
- preserve `MASTER_ARCHITECT_GUIDE_RESEARCH_BASIS.md` as non-authoritative rationale;
- require future Master Architect instances to follow the Guide until explicitly amended or superseded.

## Scope

The Guide governs Master Architect workflow: Director interaction, authority handling, delegation, context selection, anti-anchoring, synthesis, canonical maintenance, technical descent, implementation verification, handoff, and process improvement.

It does **not** choose simulation mechanics, programming languages, frameworks, runtimes, databases, AI vendors, agent products, or software architecture.

## Reversibility

High. The Guide is versioned governance and may be amended or superseded through explicit constitutional approval when project experience or better evidence justifies change.
