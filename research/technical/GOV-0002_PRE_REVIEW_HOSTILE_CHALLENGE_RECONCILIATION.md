- reviewer receives the new semantic layer, compact canonical adjacent-semantic map, invariants/exclusions, and retrieval entrypoints;

## 3. Finding B — closure staleness after later semantic change

**Challenge:** a closure can become stale if a later decision/spec change lands before acceptance freeze.

**Judgment:** VALID.

**Repair in candidate2:**

- closure binds to an exact semantic base;
- any material semantic change after closure invalidates freshness for freeze;
- explicit re-evaluation is mandatory;
- re-evaluation may be:
  - `REVALIDATED — NO FRONTIER CHANGE`; or
  - `REOPENED — AFFECTED FRONTIER`;
- only affected scope must rerun when reopening is needed.

This prevents stale closure while avoiding reflexive full reruns.
