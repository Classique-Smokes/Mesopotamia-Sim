## 1. Purpose

SFL v0 may continue to call itself a social-mechanism laboratory with the current scaffold.

Its present resource subsystem exists to make one bounded collective material capability concrete enough to test:

- grounded collective action rather than unexplained institutional power;
- separation of office authority from private resource authority;
- same-person multi-capacity behavior without semantic collapse;
- material conflict and live revalidation;
- explicit funding/effect provenance;
- Household identity and role persistence independently of one exact resource representation.

It does not establish a mature theory of Household wealth, ownership, pooling, contribution, obligation, budgeting, production, inheritance, or historical priority.

## 2. Claims boundary

### SFL v0 may support

Within the accepted scaffold, v0 may support structural/model claims that:

- a persistent Household can exercise a bounded material capability through intelligible people, authority, resources and history;
- institutional authority and private-resource authorization can remain distinct even when held by the same person;
- collective capability can alter individual possibility while remaining causally inspectable;
- resource conflicts can be resolved deterministically with explicit invalidation/revalidation;
- Household identity/role/recognition do not reduce to current contributors, current material capacity, or a Household-owned balance.

### SFL v0 must not support

Without later historical/economic research and validation, v0 must not be read as evidence that:

- Mesopotamian resources were generally personally owned;
- Households generally lacked collective/patrimonial/institutional resource claims;
- Household access normally depended on voluntary personal commitments;
- the protected reserve, fixed-rank allocator, Household-vs-debt priority, support entitlement, head authority, or private supplementation are historical norms;
- current emergent frequencies calibrate historical incidence, class structure, gendered control, wealth distribution, credit behavior, or economic efficiency;
- one period/status context generalizes across Mesopotamia.

## 3. Reversibility

The current implementation remains replaceable.

The present Slice-3 coupling is narrow; the larger lock-in risk is prospective Slice-4 implementation if funding semantics are duplicated across candidate generation, feasibility checks, dependency discovery, resolver ordering, commit mutation, and history reconstruction.

Even deliberately different future resource families—pooled Household stores, mixed personal/joint claims, or obligation/production-flow authorization—should remain resource-subsystem + resolver/history + verification migrations rather than Household identity/role or reference-kernel rewrites if the following seam is preserved.

## 4. Required guardrails

### G1 — explicit v0 / non-historical semantic boundary

The current material mechanism is a **synthetic v0 material-grounding scaffold**.

Personal grain, protected reserve / NeedsGrain exposure, HouseholdProvisionCommitment, commitment-only MobilizableCapacity, fixed-rank allocation, current conflict priorities, and transaction-specific private X are laboratory mechanisms, not future economic commitments or reconstructed Mesopotamian norms.

The durable commitment is to grounded authority/resource/obligation provenance, not these exact mechanisms.

### G2 — one authoritative live funding-resolution point

Each Household material action must have one authoritative precommit/transaction evaluation that produces the exact validated funding result.

Candidate generation may perform deliberative feasibility checks, but resolver, commit and history must not independently reimplement the funding allocator.

### G3 — explicit typed funding provenance

Successful material history must retain structured funding provenance sufficient to distinguish total cost/effect, private current-holder contribution, commitment-backed legs, source persons, backing source references, authority/consent provenance, actual debits/effects and relevant causal/rule/configuration references.

Aggregate material deltas or free-form strings are insufficient where they would lose the accepted source distinction.

### G4 — dependency discovery consumes declared material participation

Resolver/dependency discovery must derive conflicts from the action's declared/evaluated resource participation rather than duplicating allocation policy.

### G5 — standing capacity remains derived/non-authoritative

MobilizableCapacity remains rebuildable derived state. No convenience cache or balance may become independent Household wealth authority without a later accepted decision.

### G6 — resource mechanics do not define organization

Commitment presence, capacity, contributor rank, private X or resource balances do not define Household identity, sustaining participation, role identity, role occupancy or recognition identity.

### G7 — observation interpretation / sensitivity

Resource semantics are an experimental factor, not background plumbing.

Before later semantic hardening is justified by observed v0 dynamics rather than by direct authority/local correctness, run a bounded sensitivity/falsification package including:

- broad resource-regime parameter sweeps;
- same-total/different-timing schedules;
- at least one structurally different simple resource-input mechanism;
- a small interaction design over the strongest factors.

Observation reports should label claims as at least `within current v0 scaffold` or `robust across tested resource variants`.

A later replacement of the economic/resource subsystem requires rerunning materially dependent social observations.

### G8 — future redesign trigger

Reopen Household resource semantics before accepting a later subsystem that requires general claims about Household assets, ownership/control, production, pooled/common stores, inheritance, taxation/dues, labor/service obligations, budgets/accounting, differentiated goods, dependents' consumption claims, unequal asset rights, external institutional transfers, or historically calibrated resource/economic behavior.

The future research question should be framed as **who can do what with which resources/claims, under what conditions, in which period/status context**, not merely “who owns it?”
