# TRES-0011A — SFL v0 Resource Scaffold / Laboratory-Fitness Audit

**Status:** independent advisory research report  
**Role:** senior simulation-model review  
**Scope:** laboratory fitness and reversibility only; no substantive approval of the six SID decisions  
**Semantic authority:** accepted authority controls over proposal/research text

## Verdict

**LAB PURPOSE PRESERVED WITH GUARDRAILS**

The present resource/provision scaffold remains fit for SFL v0 as a social-mechanism laboratory. It is not fit to be interpreted as a historically validated household economy, a future production resource architecture, or a durable theory of Mesopotamian ownership and provisioning.

The crucial distinction is that SFL v0 is required to demonstrate one causally grounded collective material capability, not to settle the mature economic ontology of a Household. On that narrower purpose, the current model is sufficiently grounded and usefully discriminating: material action is traceable to named resources and authority; office power remains distinct from private ownership; collective capability can alter individual possibility; same-person office/private capacities can coexist without semantic collapse; conflicts are centrally revalidated; and the resulting history remains inspectable.

The model becomes misleading only if its current mechanism-specific choices are allowed to harden into conclusions about what Household property, wealth, contribution, priority, or budgeting must mean later.

This report therefore finds no laboratory-purpose reason to require a full resource redesign before Slice 4. It does require a small set of explicit semantic, verification, architecture/seam, and future-research guardrails before more economic layers are allowed to depend on this scaffold.

The six open SID questions remain unresolved until the Project Director resolves them through the existing authority process. This report evaluates only whether the current working directions can be kept laboratory-safe and reversible.

---

## 1. Authority reconstruction: what SFL v0 actually has to prove

The founding Seed establishes a long-term ambition substantially larger than the present prototype. Individuals are persistent causal actors; relationships are foundational; the Household is intended eventually to be a social, economic, property-holding, labor-organizing, and political structure; higher-order structures must remain grounded in the people, relationships, resources, norms, offices, and histories through which they operate. It also requires historical grounding without silently converting incomplete evidence into universal mechanics.

That long-term ambition does not make every future Household resource mechanism a v0 requirement.

The approved First-Prototype Development Roadmap narrows the first laboratory objective to the smallest model that can exercise the individual -> relationship -> household foundation. For the resource side, the roadmap asks for one grounded collective capability and for observable tests showing that collective capability changes support possibilities while remaining causally inspectable. The first prototype is explicitly a reference laboratory to be iterated later, not a miniature final economy.

SPEC-SFL-0001 is even more explicit. SFL v0 is a small deterministic social-mechanism laboratory. Its required demonstrations include one grounded collective material capability, one scoped representative/head role, explicit proposal/resolution, causal history, and exact continuation. Historical calibration/validation is outside the specification. Full economy/agriculture/property law is explicitly outside scope.

The architecture policy and DEC-0005 reinforce the same development discipline: start with the simplest sufficient implementation, preserve semantic authority, and avoid complexity that has not earned its cost. DEC-0006 further requires derived state to begin simply and remain replaceable behind stable semantic/query boundaries. DEC-0007 selects a deterministic reference semantic kernel designed for inspectability, revalidation, semantic history, and exact continuation rather than for final-scale production architecture.

Therefore three purposes must remain separate:

| Purpose | What is required now |
| --- | --- |
| Laboratory purpose | A causally grounded, deterministic, inspectable material seam that can exercise individual resources, collective capability, office authority, conflicts, provenance, and continuation. |
| Historical-calibration purpose | Not in v0. The current reserve size, grain quantities, allocation ordering, contribution behavior, and conflict priorities are not historical claims merely because they are executable. |
| Future production-simulation purpose | Not in v0. Future Household property, pooled assets, production, obligations, accounting, labor, differentiated control, and other resource systems may substantially replace the present mechanism. |

The correct standard is therefore not “historically final.” It is “causally grounded enough to test the structural social questions SFL v0 claims to test, while remaining visibly provisional and replaceable.”

On that standard, the resource scaffold remains fit.

## 2. Why the current scaffold is a valid laboratory mechanism

The current model is crude in several deliberate ways:

- grain is the only fungible material quantity;
- grain normally sits in personal stocks;
- a fixed protected reserve and NeedsGrain gate determine disposable exposure;
- standing Household capability is represented through explicit HouseholdProvisionCommitment relations;
- MobilizableCapacity(H) is derived from those commitments rather than stored as a Household treasury;
- actual expenditures debit real personal stocks only at commit;
- fixed-rank contribution allocation resolves who pays from standing commitments;
- DEC-0011 permits the current head to contribute an exact transaction-specific private amount X while keeping office authority and private-owner authorization distinct;
- material conflicts are revalidated at commit;
- history preserves the funding and authority provenance needed to explain what happened.

None of these mechanisms is a sufficient theory of a mature Household economy. Together, however, they form a useful v0 causal apparatus.

The scaffold forces the laboratory to answer concrete questions that a purely abstract “Household has 5 resources” counter would evade:

- Whose material capacity makes this collective act possible?
- What authority makes the act a Household act?
- Is private ownership being exposed by office power, or by separate consent?
- Can a collective action fail because its concrete material backing changed?
- Can a person act both as office-holder and private owner without treating those capacities as identical?
- Can the resulting effect be traced to the people, commitments, role, and resource state that produced it?

That is exactly the class of structural questions the founding philosophy and v0 specification require.

### 3.1 What is not essential

Several current details should be treated as consciously disposable:

- grain as the sole good;
- the exact reserve of 2;
- NeedsGrain as the only subsistence state;
- the exact fixed-rank contribution allocator;
- exposed-capacity-descending allocation order;
- stable PersonId as the exact-tie fallback;
- commitment-only standing MobilizableCapacity;
- no Household-owned store;
- head-mediated acquisition/reconsideration of provision commitment;
- the head-only transaction-specific X path;
- the current narrow spending menu;
- the current relative priority between Household expenditure and lower personal material actions.

These details may be necessary to make the reference kernel deterministic. They are not necessary conclusions about Household economics.

### 3.2 What would be premature

The present model crosses into premature semantic commitment only if a replaceable mechanism is promoted into one of the following claims:

- “Household wealth is the sum of members’ exposed personal wealth.”
- “Households do not own resources.”
- “Collective provision is fundamentally a voluntary personal-commitment system.”
- “The head is the privileged or normal private funder of Household action.”
- “Subsistence reserve is universally prior to every other obligation.”
- “Household provision is socially prior to debt repayment.”
- “Richest available contributor first is a Household allocation norm.”
- “Future Household capability should be expressed as MobilizableCapacity derived from HouseholdProvisionCommitment.”

Accepted v0 authority does not currently require any of those claims, and several are directly contradicted by the explicit exclusions and long-term openness of the project.

## 4. Distortion test: would the scaffold give a false structural answer?

The current scaffold will certainly change quantitative trajectories. Different reserve rules, pooled property, contribution systems, goods, budgets, labor obligations, or allocation rules would alter who can spend, who bears costs, how often support occurs, how often debt is repaid, and which social consequences follow.

That fact alone does not make the laboratory misleading.

The proper test is whether the current scaffold changes the structural answer to the questions SFL v0 is actually using it to ask.

### 4.1 Can a Household act through people/resources without becoming an unexplained abstract actor?

**Structural answer preserved.**

A Household action requires an Active Household, a valid occupied head role, in-scope authority, and sufficient valid transaction funding. Funding is tied to explicit standing commitments and/or an explicit private contribution term, and successful expenditure produces concrete debits with provenance.

The Household therefore has real capability, but that capability is not magic.

A future pooled Household store would change the grounding chain, but not the durable requirement: collective action must still be grounded in authoritative assets/rights/obligations and intelligible authority.

### 4.2 Can institutional authority remain separate from private ownership?

**Structural answer preserved, and DEC-0011 materially strengthens the test.**

DEC-0011 states the distinction clearly:

- role authority makes the Household act institutionally valid;
- private-owner authority makes the exact X available;
- office occupancy alone does not expose private grain;
- private ownership alone does not authorize the Household act.

This is a durable structural result even if the future resource model later contains pooled property, joint control, use rights, or Household-owned stores.

### 4.3 Can collective capability affect individual possibility?

**Structural answer preserved.**

Household material action can consume scarce personal resources, relieve NeedsGrain, enable mediated marriage through dowry, or invalidate competing material actions during central resolution. The model therefore demonstrates feedback from the collective layer into individual possibilities.

The exact frequency and distribution of those effects are not trustworthy historical results.

### 4.4 Can one person occupy office and private-owner capacities without collapse?

**Structural answer preserved.**

DEC-0011 is an explicit same-person multi-capacity test. The same actor can authorize an institutional act and separately authorize private property without inventing a second person or treating role authority as property ownership.

The D3/D4 working directions can extend the same structural discipline if their mechanism-specific wording remains v0-scoped.

### 4.5 Can material conflicts be causally inspected?

**Structural answer preserved.**

The common snapshot, explicit proposals/responses, central priority/conflict resolution, precommit revalidation, InvalidatedAtResolution outcomes, exact debit provenance, and decision/history traces provide a clear causal account.

Different conflict priorities would change outcomes. They would not invalidate the laboratory’s ability to expose why those outcomes occurred.

### 4.6 Can Household identity and role persist independently of the exact resource mechanism?

**Structural answer preserved.**

Household identity is grounded in FormationWarrant / ParticipationWarrant / ContinuationWarrant history and SustainingParticipant continuity, not in resource ownership or provision capacity. The specification explicitly states that residence, resources, attitudes, marriage changes, representative succession, and participant replacement do not by themselves break identity when continuity remains valid.

Vacancy preserves Household identity and provision commitments while suspending Household-mode action. Inactive state likewise suppresses Household-mode initiative without destroying identity.

This separation is particularly important: it means the v0 Household is not secretly defined as “the current set of contributors” or “the current resource pool.”

## 5. Where the scaffold does distort outcomes

The current scaffold is laboratory-safe only if the following distortions are treated as quantitative/mechanistic effects rather than structural findings.

### 5.1 Personal-only stocks bias the source of collective capability

Because standing capability currently comes from access to personal stocks, v0 will tend to express collective material power as a function of contributors’ private surplus.

A future Household-owned store, pooled property regime, production stream, institutional transfer, land/livestock base, or in-kind obligation could produce very different dynamics.

Therefore v0 may answer “can grounded collective capability exist?” but it must not be used to answer “where Household wealth normally comes from.”

### 5.2 The fixed reserve and NeedsGrain gate strongly shape scarcity

Reserve = 2 and the binary NeedsGrain state make personal protection highly legible, but they also make resource exposure mechanically sharp.

A more mature model may need dependents, expected consumption, status obligations, seasonality, production risk, differentiated goods, or negotiated sacrifice.

The current gate is useful for conflict testing. It is not a validated household subsistence rule.

### 5.3 Fixed-rank allocation changes who bears the cost

Sorting contributors by exposed capacity and exhausting them in fixed order is deterministic and testable, but socially artificial. It can shift later individual decisions, debts, attitudes, and needs by deciding whose grain was spent.

That does not invalidate a structural test of grounded funding. It does mean contributor-level downstream outcomes are allocator-sensitive.

The stable-ID tiebreak is correctly labeled technical fallback and must remain non-social.

### 5.4 Head-only transaction-specific supplementation can over-center the office

DEC-0011 exists to resolve a real dual-capacity seam. It should not be read as evidence that heads are historically the normal or privileged source of flexible private finance.

A future model might allow pooled property, participant contributions, earmarked funds, institutional transfers, or rights over Household assets that make the present X mechanism unnecessary.

### 5.5 Provision priority can bias debt and social consequences

Any v0 rule placing Household provision ahead of RepayDebt can determine whether repayment commits and can therefore determine whether the debt due-cycle attitude consequence later fires.

That is a real trajectory effect.

It remains laboratory-safe only if the priority is treated as a scoped deterministic conflict policy for v0, not as a historical claim that Household claims normatively outrank debt.

## 6. Future redesign compatibility

A healthy v0 scaffold should preserve durable structural conclusions while allowing the economic mechanism to be replaced.

### 6.1 Conclusions that should survive a future resource redesign

The following should remain valid even if the project later introduces pooled/common property, differentiated ownership/use/control, Household-owned stores, taxes/dues, labor/service obligations, land/livestock/production, accounting/budgets, in-kind goods, unequal asset authority, dependents, or external transfers:

1. **Collective capability must remain causally grounded.**  
   A Household may be a persistent higher-order entity, but its action must arise through intelligible authority plus authoritative resources/rights/obligations.

2. **Institutional authority and property/resource authority must remain distinguishable.**  
   An office can authorize an institutional act without thereby owning all resources used by that act.

3. **One person may carry several capacities without semantic collapse.**  
   Same-person aliasing should preserve each relevant authority/consent predicate while avoiding fake self-negotiation.

4. **Derived collective capability must not become unexplained independent truth.**  
   Whether future capability derives from balances, rights, obligations, inventories, production flows, budgets, or a combination, the derivation/provenance must remain inspectable.

5. **Material conflict must revalidate authoritative state.**  
   Future accounting may reserve or encumber resources differently, but double-spend and stale-feasibility problems cannot be hidden.

6. **Household identity, lifecycle, participation, and head-role identity must not be defined by the current resource representation.**

7. **Resource use must remain historically inspectable.**  
   The project’s genealogy requirement survives every economic redesign.

8. **Household-originated effects must not become self-confirming evidence for Household existence merely because the Household can spend resources.**

9. **Continuation/checkpoint semantics must preserve every future-influencing resource and authority state exactly enough to reproduce subsequent behavior.**

### 6.2 Conclusions that are explicitly provisional

The following should be allowed to disappear or change substantially:

- grain as the only material;
- personal stock as the only authoritative ownership location;
- reserve = 2;
- NeedsGrain as the resource-protection predicate;
- HouseholdProvisionCommitment as the standing access mechanism;
- commitment-only MobilizableCapacity;
- fixed-rank / exposed-capacity-descending contribution allocation;
- no Household treasury/store;
- head-mediated commitment solicitation;
- head-only private supplementation;
- exact X + residual R funding structure;
- the present collective-use menu;
- the present Household-vs-personal material priorities;
- any quantitative behavior produced by those choices.

A future redesign that replaces these is not contradicting the laboratory. It is using the laboratory as intended.

## 7. Six SID decisions: laboratory safety and reversibility only

This section does not approve or reject the substantive social choices. It asks only whether the current working directions can be promoted without making the v0 resource placeholder dangerously sticky.

### D1 — Household provision priority versus RepayDebt

**Classification:** resource-model-coupled but safely local; should be reworded as v0-only.

The current Option-A direction can serve as a deterministic v0 conflict rule. It gives the resolver an explicit answer and allows the laboratory to exercise resource competition and downstream debt consequences.

The risk is interpretive, not architectural: because repayment has distinct social meaning, the ordering can look like a general claim that Household provision outranks debt. It is not justified as such by v0’s purpose.

Laboratory-safe wording should make clear that the rule is a scoped SFL-v0 conflict policy needed to close deterministic resolution under the current scaffold. It should not be cited later as historical evidence or as the default priority of a future Household economy.

No future economic research is required merely to let v0 choose a deterministic result. Future research is required before this priority is generalized beyond the laboratory.

### D2 — snapshot-bound consent cohort for accepted role transition

**Classification:** resource-model-independent.

This is a temporal/consent-composition rule. It concerns which sustaining participants belong to the accepted role-transition constituency when participation changes occur in the same snapshot.

Its laboratory safety does not depend on HouseholdProvisionCommitment, grain, MobilizableCapacity, or future property mechanisms.

A future economic redesign should not require revisiting it unless the role-governance model itself changes.

### D3 — current head as needy Household-support recipient

**Classification:** resource-model-coupled but safely local.

The current working direction tests a durable multi-capacity proposition: occupying an office does not erase the holder’s ordinary person-level eligibility, and same-person role/beneficiary aliasing need not create fake self-request theatre.

The exact support mechanism is provisional. A future resource system may fund support from Household-owned assets or another channel, but the structural distinction between institutional authorization and beneficiary status can survive.

The main guardrail is to avoid wording that makes “proactive support topology” a universal future rule rather than the simplest v0 representation of this alias case.

### D4 — current head creating/reconsidering their own persistent provision commitment

**Classification:** should be reworded as v0-only. It is resource-model-coupled and is the highest hardening risk among D1–D6.

The current working direction can be laboratory-safe because it tests a useful structural point: office occupancy does not automatically expose property; the same person may separately consent in a private-owner/contributor capacity.

However, this decision directly touches the most replaceable part of the scaffold: HouseholdProvisionCommitment as the persistent standing-capability relation.

If promoted carelessly, D4 could make that relation look like a durable Household-economic primitive and could encourage later systems to build around “persistent personal commitment to Household” as the normal architecture.

The durable semantic statement should therefore be narrower than the mechanism:

- the current head may, when otherwise eligible under the v0 resource scaffold, separately and explicitly authorize the same standing personal-resource access relation available to other contributors;
- office occupancy alone does not create that access;
- no artificial self-response is required merely because one person occupies both semantic capacities;
- this says nothing about the future form of Household property, contribution, pooling, ownership, or accounting.

With that scope, D4 need not be deferred. Without it, D4 is the decision most likely to harden the placeholder accidentally.

### D5 — self-nomination for Household head

**Classification:** resource-model-independent.

This is a role/consent aliasing rule. Allowing one explicit multi-capacity act to satisfy nomination, nominee willingness, and that person’s participant acceptance does not commit the resource model.

Its future validity depends on role-governance semantics, not on the Household economy.

### D6 — head-role appointment while H is Inactive

**Classification:** resource-model-independent.

This decision separates persistent role occupancy from operational Household initiative. Inactive H may or may not fill a vacant role, while HouseholdDecisionContext remains suppressed until Active.

That distinction is compatible with either the present provision scaffold or a future pooled/accounting economy. It is fundamentally lifecycle/role semantics.

### 7.1 Net SID finding

No D1–D6 question needs to be deferred solely because the future Household economy is unknown.

D1 and especially D4 need v0-only semantic wording so a bounded resolver/commitment choice does not acquire accidental future authority. D3 should also avoid implying a permanent support-funding topology.

D2, D5, and D6 are not materially coupled to the resource scaffold.

## 8. Minimal guardrails

The following guardrails are sufficient. They are intentionally narrower than a resource redesign.

### G1 — Semantic wording guardrail

**Type:** semantic wording

When D1/D3/D4 or the relevant SPEC text is next promoted, add an explicit v0-only boundary stating that the following are laboratory mechanisms rather than future economic commitments:

- personal grain as the sole resource base;
- protected reserve / NeedsGrain exposure;
- HouseholdProvisionCommitment;
- commitment-only MobilizableCapacity;
- fixed-rank contributor allocation;
- transaction-specific head-private X;
- the present material-conflict priorities.

The durable commitment is to grounded authority/resources/provenance, not to those exact mechanisms.

### G2 — Resource seam guardrail

**Type:** architecture/seam

Do not let later layers depend directly on the storage shape or allocation internals of HouseholdProvisionCommitment.

Later code should consume only the narrow semantic services it actually needs from the v0 resource seam, such as:

- whether a scoped Household action has sufficient valid funding;
- the authoritative funding plan/provenance for commit;
- the resulting authoritative resource debits;
- whether live preconditions still validate.

MobilizableCapacity should remain rebuildable derived state, consistent with DEC-0006. No new subsystem should treat it as an independently authoritative Household wealth balance.

### G3 — Structural-decoupling verification guardrail

**Type:** verification

Keep explicit tests showing that Household identity/role semantics do not depend on one particular funding topology.

At minimum the verified matrix should include representative cases where:

- standing commitment funding is sufficient;
- a permitted transaction uses full private X with standing MobilizableCapacity = 0;
- the head and a non-head are both possible standing contributors in separate fixtures;
- a Household can remain the same Household while current provision capacity changes to zero;
- changing which contributor bears an otherwise equivalent debit may change later resource outcomes but does not rewrite Household identity, role authority, or recognition.

The purpose is not to simulate alternative future economies now. It is to prove the current economic scaffold has not become the identity/authority substrate.

### G4 — Explicit future-research trigger

**Type:** research trigger

Reopen Household resource semantics before accepting any later subsystem that requires an answer to what Household assets, ownership, contribution, or resource authority generally mean.

Triggers include, at minimum:

- Household-owned or pooled stores;
- land/livestock/production;
- inheritance;
- taxation, dues, or recurring contribution obligations;
- labor/service obligations;
- budgets/accounting;
- differentiated goods or in-kind obligations;
- dependent consumption claims;
- unequal asset control/use rights;
- external institutional transfers;
- historical calibration of wealth, provisioning, debt competition, or Household economic behavior.

Those are redesign triggers, not reasons to block Slice 4 today.

## 9. Red lines: when the scaffold has stopped being healthy

The current scaffold should be redesigned before more layers depend on it if any of the following occurs.

### Red line 1 — Resource representation starts defining Household identity

Stop if FormationWarrant, SustainingParticipant, ContinuationWarrant, lifecycle, Recognition, or head-role validity begins to depend on HouseholdProvisionCommitment, MobilizableCapacity, contributor identity, or positive resource balance except where a narrowly scoped action is checking its own feasibility.

That would collapse the intended separation between organization identity and resource mechanism.

### Red line 2 — MobilizableCapacity is treated as general Household wealth

Stop if other systems begin using MobilizableCapacity as a synonym for net worth, property, economic power, stored assets, or general budget.

Its current meaning is much narrower: standing capacity derived from one specific v0 commitment relation.

### Red line 3 — Office authority begins exposing private resources by implication

Stop if any implementation or later rule infers that head occupancy itself grants access to private grain/assets, or if a transaction-specific X is reused without explicit private-owner authorization.

That would destroy the authority/ownership separation the laboratory is meant to test.

### Red line 4 — Mechanism-specific priorities become cross-domain social law

Stop if reserve = 2, NeedsGrain exposure, richest-first allocation, Household-over-personal spending priority, or head-only supplementation is reused as a generic norm in new economic/social systems without a new authority decision.

These are v0 conflict/fixture mechanisms, not general social theory.

### Red line 5 — New domains need to interpret the commitment rather than merely query funding feasibility

Stop before adding a production, inheritance, land, livestock, taxation, labor, dependent-consumption, or accounting subsystem if that subsystem must decide what HouseholdProvisionCommitment “really means” in order to work.

That is evidence that the placeholder relation is being asked to become an economic ontology.

### Red line 6 — Replacing the resource mechanism would require rewriting Household identity or role semantics

A healthy seam should allow future pooled property or Household-owned stores to replace the funding model without redefining Household numerical identity, participation, Recognition, or the basic distinction between institutional and private authority.

If a plausible replacement requires rewriting those unrelated semantics, the current scaffold has leaked too far and should be corrected before proceeding upward.

### Red line 7 — v0 quantitative outcomes are used as historical validation

Stop if outputs such as support frequency, debt repayment rates, contributor burden, wealth distribution, Household “generosity,” head economic power, or durability under scarcity are treated as historical evidence while the current resource scaffold remains unchanged.

The specification explicitly excludes historical calibration.

### Red line 8 — The scaffold determines the structural conclusion by construction

Stop if the laboratory begins asking questions such as “are Households economically dominated by heads?” or “is individual ownership sufficient to explain Household political power?” while only the present personal-stock/head-X mechanism is available.

At that point the resource scaffold would no longer be a neutral test harness for the question; it would encode the answer.

## 10. Why a pre-Slice-4 economic redesign would be the wrong response now

A larger resource model could certainly be built before Slice 4, but doing so would solve questions SFL v0 has intentionally excluded and would violate the project’s “smallest faithful next step” discipline unless evidence shows the current mechanism is structurally misleading.

Current evidence does not show that.

The accepted model already contains strong anti-hardening properties:

- Household identity does not depend on resource ownership;
- a SustainingParticipant relation alone grants no grain access;
- a commitment does not transfer ownership before spend;
- no generic Household spending authority exists;
- no Household treasury is introduced by DEC-0011;
- office authority cannot seize personal grain;
- the transaction-specific X does not become standing capacity;
- Household-originated support/private X cannot become self-confirming organization evidence;
- exact funding provenance is retained;
- the future full economy/property law remains explicitly out of scope.

These constraints mean the scaffold is currently narrow enough to act as a laboratory instrument rather than a hidden full economic model.

The correct next move is therefore not to build pooled property, budgets, production, labor obligations, or differentiated ownership preemptively. The correct move is to keep the resource seam narrow, verify that Household identity/authority remain independent from it, and reopen the economics when a later layer genuinely needs those concepts.

---

## 11. Final assessment

The current resource scaffold changes trajectories, but it does not presently falsify SFL v0’s target structural questions.

It successfully tests that:

- a collective can possess usable capability without becoming an unexplained abstract actor;
- capability can remain grounded in lower-level authoritative state;
- institutional action authority can remain distinct from private ownership;
- one person can carry office and private-owner capacities without semantic collapse;
- collective resource use can constrain or enable individual action;
- simultaneous claims on material state can be revalidated and explained;
- Household identity and role continuity can remain independent of the exact resource-funding mechanism;
- the simulation can preserve exact causal provenance and continuation state.

The model becomes unsafe only when its mechanism-specific details are treated as durable economic conclusions or when new layers begin coupling directly to those details.

Accordingly:

- Slice 4 does not need a full Household resource redesign as a prerequisite on laboratory-fitness grounds.
- D1 and D4 should carry explicit v0-only wording if promoted; D4 deserves the strongest anti-hardening language.
- D3 is locally safe but should not imply a permanent funding topology.
- D2, D5, and D6 are resource-model-independent for present purposes.
- The resource representation should remain behind a narrow semantic seam.
- A small verification matrix should prove Household identity/role independence from funding topology.
- Future economic/property research must be triggered before any later subsystem treats the current commitment/capacity model as general Household economics.

These guardrails preserve the purpose of the scaffold: enough material structure to expose social causality now, without turning an intentionally crude v0 mechanism into tomorrow’s architecture by inertia.
