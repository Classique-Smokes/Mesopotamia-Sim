## 3. World B — relay / stale / contest

World B also ran 12 cycles with no fault or material deadlock.

Observed personal selections:

- `Farm`: 47;
- `OfferGift`: 18;
- `CommunicateClaim`: 6.

No technical fallback was used.

The mechanically observed epistemic sequence was:

1. cycles 1–2: Aru and Bela exchange direct residence facts;
2. cycle 2: both become `Recognized` for C1;
3. cycle 4: Aru communicates recognized C1 evidence to Dagan;
4. cycle 4: Dagan becomes `Recognized`;
5. cycle 6: Bela moves from D1 to D2 through the ordinary public residence-transition path;
6. Bela immediately becomes `Unknown` because her own current evidence no longer supports shared residence;
7. Aru and Dagan remain stale `Recognized` because they receive no new evidence yet;
8. cycle 8: Bela communicates her newer D2 residence fact to Dagan;
9. Dagan becomes `Unknown` because the new evidence displaces the older event-ordered D1 report and no longer supports the formation predicate;
10. cycle 9: Samu transmits an incomparable stale D1 report;
11. Dagan retains both incompatible Residence reports and becomes `Contested`;
12. cycle 10: Dagan's attempted Recognition communication to Enna is categorically gated out as `PropositionNotHeld`;
13. cycle 11: Bela communicates her newer D2 residence fact to Aru;
14. Aru becomes `Unknown`; Dagan remains `Contested` because his incomparable stale report remains.

This demonstrates the causal chain Slice 2 was meant to make executable:

objective fact
→ actor-specific evidence
→ recognition
→ stale recognition
→ newer correction
→ incomparable conflict
→ contested recognition
→ changed candidate availability.

## 5. Interpretation of “rustic”

The simulation is still visibly rustic in two separable senses.

### A. Reference personal policy remains narrow

Autonomous behavior still overwhelmingly selects `Farm` and `OfferGift`.

This is the same family of limitation observed after Slice 1. It is not evidence that the lower-level social or epistemic substrate is wrong.

### B. Epistemic mechanics are richer than epistemic behavior

The engine can represent:

- sparse actor-specific knowledge;
- provenance-bearing communication;
- stale state;
- correction;
- contested recognition;
- action gating.

But the reference policy does not independently decide when socially meaningful communication should occur.

This is deliberate and remains future richer-agency / Social Epistemic Dynamics work.

## 6. Slice-3 implications

The observation pause does not add new Slice-3 semantics.

It does make one design constraint particularly vivid:

> Household formation must remain downstream of actor-held evidence and recognition rather than being inferred from objective graph structure alone.

World A is a direct cautionary example: objective co-residence + durable ties + repeated support did not produce recognition because the actors lacked the required subjective evidence.

World B shows why formation/continuity machinery must preserve actor-specific evidence paths rather than replacing them with engine-global household truth.
