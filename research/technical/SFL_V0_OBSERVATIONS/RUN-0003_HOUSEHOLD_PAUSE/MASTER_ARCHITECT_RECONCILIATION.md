## 2. Material lessons retained

### A. Reference-policy reachability remains much narrower than substrate capability

In Mode A, all 72 personal initiatives selected `Farm`; no qualifying support, communication, CandidateRecognition, or Household formation became reachable.

This is retained as a **reference-policy limitation**.

It does not imply the Household machinery is unreachable through valid public paths. Mode B demonstrated production formation and downstream Household behavior once ordinary support and communication were supplied.

**Future consequence:** do not use autonomous activation frequency as the primary correctness probe for Slice 4. Acceptance and bounded activation-assisted narratives remain necessary until richer agency exists.

### B. Organizational knowledge is actor-causal, not status-derived

Current sustaining participation did not itself grant observation of every continuation event. Terminal dissolution evidence belonged to the terminal exiting participant. Other actors required an accepted evidence route such as communication.

**Future consequence:** role/head Recognition must preserve actor-specific evidence provenance. Current membership, office occupancy, or proximity must not become implicit omniscience.

### C. Stable Household identity is a clean anchor for office work

H remained numerically stable through participation change, founder turnover, Inactive state, and final dissolution. Continuation warrants made the identity story more legible than endpoint reconstruction.

Controlled lineage remained separate from identity.

**Future consequence:** Slice 4 should attach a persistent head role to persistent H rather than infer office identity from current holder/member snapshots. Role continuity and Household continuity must remain distinct.

### D. Prospective actors belong in dependency analysis before they hold the role/status

The residence/admission case remained the clearest implementation warning: the newcomer was not yet a participant, but its residence transition already changed same-cycle admission validity.

**Future consequence:** Slice-4 dependency/revalidation design must include prospective nominees/holders and other actors whose same-cycle state can affect appointment, succession, authority validity, or eligibility. Current-holder/current-member lookups alone are insufficient.

### E. Material backing and institutional authority remain distinct

Provision capacity changed through personal grain and commitment state without becoming a treasury or generic spending right.

**Future consequence:** head authority must remain scoped and must not make Household backing equivalent to resource ownership or general sovereignty.

### F. Causal explanation needs evidence freshness, not only final Recognition status

A status-only view could not explain why a positive Recognition was stale versus newly updated. Evidence identity, origin and communication hops made the distinction legible.

**Future consequence:** Slice-4 head/succession traces and verification should expose provenance/freshness sufficient to explain stale predecessor Recognition and accepted correction routes.

### G. Observation tooling terminology can conflate two fallback concepts

`DecisionTrace.TechnicalFallback` described deterministic personal-score ties in Mode A while `SemanticEvent.TechnicalFallback` represented central-resolution technical symmetry.

No semantic bug was established.

**Future consequence:** human-facing Slice-4 summaries should distinguish scoring/tie-selection fallback from semantic resolution fallback.

### H. Long integration narratives can be confounded by lower-slice material maintenance

The long assisted narrative depleted grain before planned positive reactivation and one intended loan-control step.

**Future consequence:** Slice-4 learning/verification narratives should use short episodes or explicitly budget the material horizon. Do not interpret later unavailable actions as authority failure when lower-level resource prerequisites have already disappeared.
