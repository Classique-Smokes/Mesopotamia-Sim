| Test | Inputs | Result | What the result does not establish |

| Reading flagged in S05 excerpt | NN 44: 40 gin; NN 46: 10 gin | 50 gin | A unique room plan, a new collation, or proof that no other property transaction occurred |
| Final partner transfer | One partner’s interest in the joint 50-gin property | Share transferred; precise fraction not independently established here | An automatic 25-gin half-share or an identified resident replacement |

These are transparent calculations from published quantities, not new readings or measurements. In the area reckoning used here, one sar comprises 60 gin; no conversion into persons or household size is made. [S01–S02; S05 excerpt.]

## H. Interpretation audit: propositions deliberately not adopted

**Household size:** No estimate from floor area, room count, number of tablet fragments, or number of named heirs.

**Kinship:** No kin relationship inferred solely from proximity, a shared witness, a name resemblance, or burial in the same general building area.

**Residence:** No owner, seller, buyer, witness, tenant of an unidentified property, archive subject, or seal owner automatically classified as a resident of the find-building.

**Teaching:** No model contract counted as an operative transaction; no teacher’s family inferred from a later legal deposit; no pupil count from fragment totals.

**Social status:** No small house automatically classified as poor, large house as an extended family, female-inscribed seal as proof of an unmarried religious resident, or TB property as institutionally owned solely from a lack of sale deeds.

**Continuity:** No persistence of walls equated with persistence of a household; no sequence of ownership treated as a sequence of inhabitants without separate evidence.

**Causation:** No burial concentration treated as evidence of famine, epidemic, warfare, or a particular mortality rate without the relevant independent evidence.

**Historical reach:** No local TA/TB endpoint applied to all Nippur; no evidence from another city or period silently moved into the target frame.

These exclusions do not assert that any proposed historical arrangement was impossible. They identify which arrangements the evidence examined in this task does not establish.
