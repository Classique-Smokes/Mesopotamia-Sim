# RES-0001A — Source Map

**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Scope:** Old Babylonian Nippur, ca. 1900–1600 BCE, with explicitly marked external and comparative material.  
**Status:** Advisory research aid; no design decisions. This is not an exhaustive census of tablets, houses or archives.

S-identifiers resolve to the complete ledger in `RES-0001A_RESULT.md`, §9. A-C identifiers point to its evidence table. The matrices distinguish actually examined material from routes that still require direct consultation. A blank or unknown findspot is not silently filled with a plausible house.
