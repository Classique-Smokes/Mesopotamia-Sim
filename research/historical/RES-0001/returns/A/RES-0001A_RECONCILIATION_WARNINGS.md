## Workstream handoff

**B — Kinship, Marriage, Membership; C — Adoption, Inheritance, Continuity:** The clearest contribution is a distinction between text-level relationships and reconstructed genealogies. A-C06–A-C10 and A-C22 identify the evidence chain and its limits; they do not decide the substantive family-history questions.

**D — Property, Resources, Debt, Obligation; E — Labor, Dependency, Slavery, Service:** TX01–TX03 and TX08–TX09 are bounded legal examples. The report does not derive citywide wealth distributions, rates of dependency, employment patterns or household production profiles from them. S10 is a consequential uncompleted administrative route.

**F — Domestic Archaeology, Residence, Neighborhood:** HC01–HC08 identify the architectural routes and the points at which original plans, phase correlations and formation processes require checking. The present research is not an independent reconstruction of all houses.

**G — Household–Institution Interfaces:** HC04, HC09 and LC01–LC02 distinguish office/document custody, administrative coverage and external institutional continuity. Neither “private” nor “temple” should be read here as a complete description of every person’s social setting.

**H — Terminology, Historical Categories:** A-C10, A-C18 and A-C22 distinguish taught formulations, lexical comparisons and event-specific legal relationships. Existing translations remain attributed and uncertain meanings remain open.

## Matters not resolved by this handoff

The precise final-abandonment sequence, full House F debate, complete Mannum-mešu-liṣṣur genealogy, detailed Jena administrative organization, and the secure replacement origins of the disputed market tablets remain unadjudicated. Their unresolved status is substantive information, not permission to select whichever reconstruction is simplest.

**No pre-harmonization:** Other agents’ results were not consulted or rewritten. Agreement among future packets must be checked for shared underlying sources before it is treated as independent corroboration.
