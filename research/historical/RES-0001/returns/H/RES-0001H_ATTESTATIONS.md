# RES-0001H — Contextual attestation register

**Accessed:** 19 September 2026. **Purpose:** Audit trail for the research return, not a simulation taxonomy.

Twenty-five direct digital ancient-text records, one quarantined association, and one metadata-only legal lead are distinguished below. Forms are abbreviated reading aids, not fresh collations. Brackets/question marks preserve uncertainty.

## T01 — BE 6/2, 28

**Object:** CBS 11173; [P266334](https://cdli.earth/P266334). **Date:** Samsuiluna 8, month III.  
**Context:** Nippur; no specific room/archive established here. **Genre:** Adoption, allocation and maintenance provisions.  
**Access / language:** DIRECT_DIGITAL_TEXT; Sumerian clauses; digital English translation also consulted.  
**Passages / forms:** obv. 1–18; rev. 1–8; nam-ibila; ad-da ti-la; dumu; šeš; ḫa-la; é du₃-a; a₂ mu-u₈-a; kalag; nam-ibila-ni-ta ... e₁₁.  
**Observation:** The document combines heir-making, a living father’s allocations, brother terminology and conditional annual support.  
**Limit:** Damaged adoption wording and obv. 9 are not silently restored. No proof of biological brotherhood, co-residence or performance of the annual obligations.  
**Concordance:** MC 3, 04; P283600 is retired, not another tablet.  
**Design status:** NO DESIGN DECISION

## T02 — SAOC 44, 01

**Object:** IM 058763; [P224324](https://cdli.earth/P224324). **Date:** Rim-Sin 30, month VII.  
**Context:** Nippur; 3N-T0852; room not given in consulted record. **Genre:** Marriage and affiliation provisions.  
**Access / language:** DIRECT_DIGITAL_TEXT; Sumerian legal clauses with some Akkadian elements.  
**Passages / forms:** obv. 1–18; rev. 1–4; nam-dam-še₃ ... tuku; dam; dumu; ad-da; nam-dumu.  
**Observation:** Marriage and the affiliation of named sons appear in one instrument; repudiation clauses identify spouse and parent relationships.  
**Limit:** DAM must be interpreted relationally, not invariably as wife. Conditional penalties are not reports that a sale or repudiation occurred.  
**Concordance:** MC 3, 14.  
**Design status:** NO DESIGN DECISION

## T03 — PBS 8/2, 116

**Object:** CBS 07112; [P262151](https://cdli.earth/P262151). **Date:** Rim-Sin 50, month II.  
**Context:** Nippur; specific excavation locus not recorded here. **Genre:** Sister-maintenance agreement; tablet and envelope.  
**Access / language:** DIRECT_DIGITAL_TEXT; Sumerian; digital English translation consulted.  
**Passages / forms:** tablet obv. 1–13, rev. 1–16; envelope obv. 1–15; nin₉; lukur; šeš-gal; ibila; nam-ibila; še-ba; ì-ba; siki-ba; é a-šà níĝ-gur₁₁.  
**Observation:** Four brothers/heirs undertake recurrent provision for their sister Bēltani, LUKUR of Ninurta; non-provision and alienation clauses affect heirship/property.  
**Limit:** The field possessive in the alienation provision is contested in scholarship. Allocation vocabulary is not by itself a wage relation. Tablet and envelope constitute one case.  
**Design status:** NO DESIGN DECISION
