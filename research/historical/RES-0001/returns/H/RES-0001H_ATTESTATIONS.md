# RES-0001H — Contextual attestation register

**Accessed:** 19 September 2026. **Purpose:** Audit trail for the research return, not a simulation taxonomy.

Twenty-five direct digital ancient-text records, one quarantined association, and one metadata-only legal lead are distinguished below. Forms are abbreviated reading aids, not fresh collations. Brackets/question marks preserve uncertainty.
