## How to interpret the records

D-C identifiers are local claim labels; D-K identifiers label selected document/case groups; D-R identifiers distinguish matrix rows. They are research references, not proposed simulation structures or canonical historical-register numbers. S01–S18 identify sources.

Multiple matrix rows can refer to a single document. Tablet and envelope do not count as separate events. CSV counts are package-description counts, not statistics about ancient Nippur.

CSV files use UTF-8 with a byte-order mark for common spreadsheet importers. Dates remain regnal strings; automatic conversion into Gregorian dates would be misleading. Missing or unresolved values are stated in words, not set to zero.

## Important limits

The research is strongest for property division/transfers, maintenance, specified loan terms, and distinctions between conditional and recorded legal outcomes. It does not establish citywide wealth, normal interest rates, automatic household liability, or a primary-verified actual guarantee/collateral-enforcement sequence.

The full text/plans of Stone 1987 and the original Hunter 1930 / Stone–Owen 1991 editions were not obtained. Three archaeological/institutional loan leads are provisional, and the gift/compensation cases remain secondary-mediated. The main report specifies these limits in its opening, methods, analysis, and source ledger.

No copyrighted source PDFs, article transcriptions, or font files are included. The package is an original research synthesis with traceable documentary references. Original task instructions and planning files remain separate and unchanged.
