# RES-0001B — Relationship-process matrix

**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Scope:** Selected Old Babylonian Nippur evidence; predominantly eighteenth-century cases.  
**Status:** Advisory research; **NO DESIGN DECISION**.

This is the complete matrix also included in §4.9 of `RES-0001B_RESULT.md`. D identifiers refer to the documentary inventory, B-C identifiers to the main report’s evidence table and S identifiers to its source ledger. “Direct/documented” describes what is available in the stated source-access tier, not a claim of new tablet collation. The matrix does not define canonical household membership.

HIGH / MODERATE / LOW are confidence labels for bounded claims. UNRESOLVED is a research status: no confident historical transition is inferred. Conditional provisions, catalogue descriptions, explicit events and modern reconstructions must not be counted interchangeably.
