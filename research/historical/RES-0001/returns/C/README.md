## Important limits to carry into review

The complete Stone/Owen 1991 and Goddeeris 2016 volumes, the full Meinhold 2020 chapter, and the architectural content of Stone 1987 were not accessed. Selected underlying texts and later published re-editions/collations were read where the ledgers say so. The court tablet has an excavation identifier, but a secure house/phase association was not established here. Neither principal case is presented as a completely verified excavated residential archive.

Female adoptees and adopted enslaved heirs are not established by this sample. Nor does it establish adoption rates, normal household size, complete care performance or a universal inheritance rule. The actual date distribution is narrower than the approved c.1900–1600 BCE frame. These are explicit boundaries, not evidence that the missing practices did not exist.

For scholarly review, prioritize the revised date of MC 3, 51, the identity/reading problems in OECT 8 seals and names, and the competing interpretations of TMH 10, 18. The full treatment is in report sections 4–5. Questions for additional research are in section 7; proposed register entries remain candidates in section 8.
