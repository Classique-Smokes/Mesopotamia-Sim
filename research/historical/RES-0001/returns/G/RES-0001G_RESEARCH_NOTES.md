# RES-0001G — Research Notes and Verification Queue

**Date:** 19 September 2026  
**Purpose:** An auditable source/case trail and unresolved issues. These are research observations and retrieval notes, not private reasoning, a transcript of every search, or additional canonical claims.

## 1. Completed operations

The seven files of the assigned task package were read/extracted separately from the original archive. The shared protocol and return template governed the deliverables. Web discovery followed the supplied bibliography into old editions, object catalogues and later specialist discussion. Relevant passages were read rather than treating search snippets as full editions. The positive case list was reduced to identifiable documents with an explicit access tier. Control material was kept out of the positive Nippur count.

The generated JSON is a research export of the report’s cases, propositions and sources, not a proposed simulation data structure. No other task’s results were imported. Original project files were not edited.

## 3. Controls and quarantined leads

**BE 6/2 29:** the field lease names individuals and a divinely named field. Neither alone establishes institutional ownership or the tenant’s membership in a temple household. It is a classification control, not a positive institutional case. [S01, pp. 43–44]

**BE 6/2 51:** a hire arrangement involves a named worker, his father and another individual. It is not classified as temple employment merely from personal names. [S01, p. 43]

**BE 6/2 4:** Poebel’s adoption translation includes a debated religious-status/sustenance passage. It was not promoted into the positive women/prebend evidence because interpretation and provenance/date verification require a modern check. Web reproductions using confident sexual or occupational labels were not used as scholarly authorities. [S01, pp. 31–32]

**BE 6/2 46:** seal inscriptions were encountered, but the complete operative adoption text was not studied. It is not counted as an additional analyzed adoption case. A seal/name hint is not a reconstructed transaction.

**BE 6/2 6 and 47:** conflicting provenance notes make them unsuitable for unqualified Nippur inclusion in this pass. Nos. 8–9 and non-Nippur appendix material were excluded. [S01, introduction/catalogue]

**CDLI P258016 / BE 6/2 82 (date only):** explicit non-Nippur control; no imported exchange terms. [S11]

**OBCN I 43, 45 and 69:** potential additional office exchanges/recovery and a fragment involving more than one city were located in S02. They remain catalogue leads, not accepted reconstructions of a new family or cross-city institutional network. No. 45’s own uncertainty must not be resolved by preference.

**CBS7528 / TMH 10 168:** S03 contains an apparent cross-reference issue between a footnote and separately listed table entries. Without checking the full TMH edition and images, this return neither asserts identity nor declares an erratum. These entries were not used as distinct positive cases.

**Cotsen 52175:** didactic and unprovenanced/proposed Larsa sphere, not a Nippur event. [S05]

## 5. Candidate Nuska chain: explicit acceptance test

G-C15 remains LOW. Before linking BE 6/2 37 with OBCN I 32, a comparison must establish the relevant personal identities (including patronymics), compatible temple and office descriptions, a matching or explainable term, and consistency between the earlier exchange and the later retrospective description. Similar names plus a similar temple are not sufficient. Even a successful match would prove one asset history, not a general cycle of office speculation.

## 6. What is complete and what remains open

Complete: the nine-section independent return; 14-row interface table; 16 claim entries; eight structured candidate claims; access-qualified source ledger; explicit comparisons, exclusions and research questions; package and consistency checks.

Open: image collation; selected exact dates/museum concordances; modern legal re-editions; a full remuneration study; secure residential association; complete administrative network or prosopographic reconstruction; matched social/chronological comparison. These are not treated as secretly completed work.
