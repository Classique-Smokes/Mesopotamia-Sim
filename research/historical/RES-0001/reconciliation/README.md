# RES-0001R — Reconciliation Return

This package reconciles the eight independent RES-0001 Old Babylonian Nippur research returns into an advisory, deduplicated evidence set for Master Architect review.

## Contents

- `01_MASTER_PROPOSITION_SET.md` — 43 consolidated `HEC-` propositions with provenance, evidence character, disagreements, confidence, limits, and `NO DESIGN DECISION` status.
- `02_EVIDENCE_CONCORDANCE.csv` — proposition-to-evidence mapping at the level of ancient documents, contexts, dossiers, and archaeological associations.
- `03_DISPUTE_AND_QUARANTINE_REGISTER.md` — unresolved readings, provenance problems, reconstruction limits, source-access issues, didactic-text controls, and two quarantined source candidates.
- `04_CANDIDATE_CLAIM_CROSSWALK.csv` — complete 69/69 disposition crosswalk from returns A–H.
- `05_RECONCILIATION_QA.md` — integrity, duplicate-source, source-access, conflict, coverage, and design-language QA.

## Reconciliation result

- Input returns examined: **8/8**.
- Register-ready source candidates inventoried: **69**.
- Consolidated reconciliation propositions: **43**.
- Source-candidate dispositions: **58 represented by HEC propositions, 6 redundant, 2 quarantined, 3 not register-worthy**.

`HEC-` numbers are reconciliation candidate identifiers only. They are **not** canonical `HE-` Historical Evidence Register numbers and do not constitute project decisions.

**Authority:** advisory only. **Design status:** `NO DESIGN DECISION`.
