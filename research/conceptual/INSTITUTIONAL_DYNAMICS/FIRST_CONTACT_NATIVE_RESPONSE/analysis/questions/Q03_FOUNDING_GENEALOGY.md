# Q03 — Who Made the Conservatory?

**Surface coverage:** INDIRECT  
**Analytical stage:** native extraction only; no Uruk comparison

## Delivered question

If someone insists on asking who founded the Conservatory, which people, agents, practices, influences, artifacts, decisions, accidents, or later acts of acceptance matter?

## Native material in the response

The handoff never names a founder.

Instead it distributes causal contribution:

- **pp.22–25:** the Game Director holds final creative authority and determines when directions become official.
- **pp.35–38:** the Threadkeeper/high-context AI performs design-history reconstruction and integration; implementation agents perform substantial engineering and repository authoring; the Game Director remains persistent creative authority.
- **p.38:** the high-context design role can make repository documentation changes when explicitly asked, but should not silently mutate gameplay/design state.
- **pp.39–47:** the continuity layer is narrated as a response to accumulated failure history, especially the external-handoff overload episode.
- **p.47:** the concrete response is a bundle of durable artifacts and routing/protection rules.
- **pp.47–52:** the new machinery is characterized as lightweight, partly inherited from older practice, and justified by observed problems.
- **pp.66–69:** the project's historical development is narrated through work episodes rather than a founder event.

## Native-position reconstruction

**Inference from the response:**

The handoff resists a single-person founding account by construction.

Its causal story is closer to:

**existing Wonder Gather practices and scars -> external handoff exposes a coordination bottleneck -> project responds by externalizing state/rationale and naming collaboration roles -> human authority and repository changes make the layer durable.**

The document gives the Game Director decisive creative/acceptance authority, but it does not say that final authority equals sole authorship.

It gives the high-context AI a strong interpretive/integration role, but explicitly denies that mnemonic role-names themselves create authority.

It gives repository artifacts durable causal importance without treating the repository as the creative sovereign.

## What the response does not establish

The response does not identify:

- who coined the Conservatory metaphor;
- who authored each continuity document;
- who selected the final role vocabulary;
- which foreign actors or artifacts contributed specific concepts;
- whether later acceptance should count as founding;
- whether the term `founder` is useful at all.

Those omissions are real non-answers, not blanks to be silently filled from Uruk's private event record during the native-extraction stage.
