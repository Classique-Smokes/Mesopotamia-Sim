## Native-position reconstruction

**Inference from the response:**

The handoff's strongest durable inception marker is the merge of the **Conservatory continuity layer** at `d03e0456`.

But the same document also roots that layer in older practices and failures:

- durable design authority existed earlier in GAME_VISION;
- Validation had already accumulated failure memory;
- isolated implementation contexts already protected human work;
- the high-context AI already performed continuity/integration work before the Conservatory label;
- the external-handoff overload episode supplied a concrete trigger for new externalization.

So the response implicitly distinguishes at least:

1. **older practices/functions**;
2. **the failure that exposed a coordination need**;
3. **the newly named/documented continuity layer**;
4. **the merge that made that layer durable in the repository**.

It does not itself decide which of those counts as "the beginning".
