# Q05 — Luis the Person and the Keeper of the Horizon

**Surface coverage:** DIRECT-LEANING INDIRECT  
**Analytical stage:** native extraction only; no Uruk comparison

## Delivered question

Which parts of Wonder Gather's continuity depend on Luis as a particular person rather than on the abstract Keeper function? If another person inherited identical formal authority but had different taste, memory, habits, and values, what could remain continuous and what could change?

## Native material in the response

The handoff draws a strong distinction between the **optional title** and the underlying human authority.

- **p.22:** the human owner/Game Director has final authority over game design, creative direction, priorities, whether an experiment feels right, whether design statuses become Locked, and experiential milestone acceptance.
- **p.22:** `Keeper of the Horizon` is explicitly described as newer cultural shorthand; **the title is optional, the authority is not**.
- **pp.23–25:** implementation agents may decide bounded engineering details, but unresolved game design and experiential questions require human approval.
- **p.37:** the Keeper is again described as the human role controlling creative authority, milestone priority, experiential acceptance, design locking/unlocking, and whether a technically valid outcome belongs in Wonder Gather.
- **p.37:** this role is intentionally not automated away.
- **p.52:** the main scarce resource is creative integration: "Does this technically plausible thing still belong to Wonder Gather?" That judgment currently belongs to the Game Director.
- **pp.60–62:** disappearance of the Game Director is called the most severe continuity loss. The repository can preserve decisions and reasoning but cannot authoritatively decide whether movement feels alive, UI feels inviting, a compromise belongs aesthetically, or the game still expresses its intended emotional center.

## Native-position reconstruction

The handoff treats two things as distinct:

1. **the role/title/authority structure**; and
2. **the particular human creative judgment currently occupying that position**.

The Keeper name is dispensable. The final creative authority and experiential judgment are not.

**Inference from the response:** if another person inherited identical formal authority, substantial project continuity could remain through repository state, locked design pillars, implementation, validation, and the authority structure itself. But the future interpretation of "belongs in Wonder Gather" could change because the current project explicitly treats high-context human taste and emotional judgment as irreducible to the artifacts.

The document therefore does not model the Keeper as a fungible administrator.

## What the response does not establish

It does not answer:

- whether a successor with different taste would still be recognized as continuing the same creative project;
- how much divergence would be tolerated before continuity breaks;
- whether Luis's personal values are constitutive of Wonder Gather or merely current authority;
- who could legitimately judge continuity after such a succession.
