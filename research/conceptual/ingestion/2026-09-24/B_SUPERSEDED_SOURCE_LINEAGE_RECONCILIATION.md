#### B. Institutional age is not elapsed time

The older source explicitly distinguishes calendar age from accumulated/integrated work and gives a transmission ladder:

`labor performed -> information produced -> artifact preserved -> artifact retrieved -> information interpreted -> information incorporated -> later behavior changed`.

V2 omits this entire excursion. The current derived program, however, has already promoted the underlying distinction:

- `00_PROGRAM_THESIS.md` states **Institutional age is not elapsed time** and separates elapsed time, accumulated labor, successions, consequential change density, and maturity;
- `01_CANDIDATE_MECHANISM_MODEL.md` family M preserves institutional age/social periodization;
- family H plus the externalized-causality thesis preserve the fact that storage alone is insufficient: artifacts must remain accessible/recognized/adopted and must later affect actors.

The older ladder is therefore a useful **sharpening/example** of already-derived machinery, not a missing family.

Disposition: **ABSORBED — EXISTING HOME / REDUNDANT CORE — SOURCE RETAINED**.

#### C. Four simultaneous histories

The older source explicitly enumerates actual causal history, official institutional history, current participant memory, and outsider reconstruction. V2 compresses this distinction. Institutional Dynamics L now preserves the same four-way separation almost verbatim at mechanism level.

Disposition: **ABSORBED — EXISTING HOME**.

## 5. Cross-source reconciliation

The two restored predecessors converge on one broad pattern already present in the current derived program:

1. causal work is performed by concrete actors;
2. repeated work/failure/transmission creates durable procedures, artifacts, roles, and dependencies;
3. those structures shape later actors after the original contributors are absent or forgotten;
4. formal title, practical centrality, authority, recognition, memory, and welfare can diverge;
5. later observers may inherit a compact, socially authoritative structure whose full causal path is no longer visible.

This is already covered by Institutional Dynamics' **externalized causality** plus mechanism families H (artifacts/documentary memory), I (procedures/practices), J (tasks/responsibility/skill/knowledge), K (effective centrality), L (multiple genealogies/archival rupture), and M (institutional age/social periodization). No additional umbrella is justified.
