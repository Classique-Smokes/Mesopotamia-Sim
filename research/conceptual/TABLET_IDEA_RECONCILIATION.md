# New Tablet Collections — Idea Reconciliation

**Status:** MASTER ARCHITECT SYSTEMATIZATION / NON-AUTHORITATIVE  
**Sources:** `THE_ROADS_BETWEEN_PEOPLE_THIRD_JESTER_COMMISSION/`, `THE_RETIRED_VIZIER_TABLETS_FIRST_COLLECTION/`  
**Purpose:** Distinguish current Slice-2 lessons, genuinely new later ideas, meaningful overlap with existing future programs, and truly redundant conceptual payload.

## 1. Classification rule

This reconciliation classifies **ideas**, not whole tablets.

A single tablet may contain:

- a core distinction already established elsewhere;
- a new extension to that distinction;
- a current Slice-2 lens;
- and a much later research question.

Therefore:

- **REDUNDANT CORE** means the tablet restates a distinction already durably represented and does not require a new concept family for that portion.
- **OVERLAP — CONSOLIDATE** means the tablet adds useful causal texture to an existing family. The material should be cross-linked and found together, not treated as duplicate or erased.
- **SLICE-2 LENS** means the idea can sharpen current planning/verification/observation without creating new Slice-2 semantics.
- **LATER NEW** means the tablet introduces a genuinely distinct future question not already represented adequately.
- **PROJECT/GOVERNANCE ANALOGY** means the observation may be valuable for this project’s process but is not yet a simulation concept.

No classification here promotes observer material into accepted simulation authority.

## 3. Roads corpus — future concept disposition

### 3.1 Mediated relationship ancestry

**Source:** `THE_MAN_INTRODUCED_BY_ANOTHER_MAN.md`

**Finding:** **LATER NEW.**

A later direct relationship may have causal prehistory involving third parties who are not members of the current dyad.

This is not adequately captured by merely recording the dyad's own events.

**Primary home:** `SOCIAL_EPISTEMIC_DYNAMICS/`

**Related existing ideas:** objective causal genealogy / provenance, but this case operates below institutional genealogy and should not be subsumed into it.

### 3.2 Group information environments without group minds

**Source:** `THE_HOUSE_THAT_KNEW_A_MAN_WHO_NEVER_ENTERED_IT.md`

**Finding:** **OVERLAP — CONSOLIDATE.**

Already established:
- higher-order structures must not erase individuals;
- actor-specific knowledge must not globally synchronize;
- household/institutional recognition is individual and provenance-bearing.

New emphasis:
- repeated co-presence plus asymmetric external ties can make a group function as an information environment while members retain different beliefs/reasons.

**Primary home:** Social Epistemic Dynamics.

**Cross-link:** SFL Slice 3 household work and Institutional Dynamics' higher-order grounding/recognition questions.

Do not create a primitive `HouseholdBelief` concept from this story.

### 3.3 Distributed social representation / reputation

**Source:** `THE_WOMAN_EVERYONE_HAD_HEARD_OF.md`

**Finding:** **LATER NEW.**

Existing future material mentions reputation only incidentally and explicitly excludes a one-scalar shortcut. It does not yet contain a dedicated model/question family for many actor-held representations of one person feeding back into later behavior.

**Primary home:** Social Epistemic Dynamics.

Future questions:
- actor-specific representations of a person;
- propagation beyond direct acquaintance;
- contradictory representations across network regions;
- feedback from reputation-shaped treatment into later behavior;
- correction attempts becoming new reputation evidence.

Do not reduce this to a global reputation score.

### 3.4 Brokerage / informational centrality

**Source:** `THE_BRIDGE_EVERYONE_NEEDED.md`

**Finding:** **OVERLAP — CONSOLIDATE, NOT REDUNDANT.**

Existing Institutional Dynamics already contains:
- Effective centrality / influence;
- brokerage between groups;
- unique knowledge;
- communication/work flows;
- the Two-Ledger Integrator challenge.

The Roads tablet adds:
- bypass cost as a source of power;
- social translation/interpretation as brokerage;
- dependency created by repeated route use;
- burden and discretion produced by the same topology;
- hidden dependencies revealed when the broker disappears.

**Primary conceptual family:** Institutional Dynamics K — Effective centrality / influence.

**Sibling lens:** Social Epistemic Dynamics — brokerage and informational centrality.

These are the **same broader family viewed from different levels**, not two competing mechanisms. Both programs should cross-link here.

### 3.5 Plural network memory / social worlds

**Source:** `THE_TWO_QUARTERS_THAT_REMEMBERED_DIFFERENT_FIRES.md`

**Finding:** **OVERLAP — CONSOLIDATE WITH GENUINE EXTENSION.**

Existing Institutional Dynamics already preserves:
- actor-specific recognition;
- multiple genealogies;
- outsider reconstruction;
- official versus causal history;
- Foreign Observer challenge.

The Roads tablet adds:
- clustered living transmission before formal institutional memory;
- trust-weighted local coherence;
- later events reorganizing remembered earlier events;
- network structure becoming more segregated because of the histories it carries.

**Primary home:** Social Epistemic Dynamics — plural network memory.

**Cross-link:** Institutional Dynamics L — Multiple genealogies and archival rupture.

The institutional version is a special higher-order application of a broader social-epistemic process.

## 4. Retired Vizier corpus — future concept disposition

### 4.1 Office separate from holder

**Sources:** *The Seal Was Removed, but the Man Remained*; *The Vizier Who Survived His Office*

**Finding:** **REDUNDANT CORE.**

Already durably established:
- office/role separate from holder;
- formal office distinct from effective influence;
- succession preserves office while changing occupant;
- former-holder influence may differ from current authority.

Do not create a new mechanism family merely for these base distinctions.

The source remains useful as a challenge/example.

### 4.2 Role-conditioned salience / institutional internalization

**Source:** `THE_SEAL_WAS_REMOVED_BUT_THE_MAN_REMAINED.md`

**Finding:** **LATER NEW.**

The existing program distinguishes attention/motivation/capacity but does not adequately represent the idea that sustained responsibility can train **what becomes salient before conscious choice**.

Important boundary:
- the same adaptation may create both expertise and blindness.

Possible later home:
- richer individual agency / attention;
- Work, Knowledge and Coordination;
- Offices/Roles when role-conditioned cognition becomes relevant.

Do not model this as a simple office bonus/penalty scalar.

### 4.3 Post-office social succession / predecessor shadow

**Source:** `THE_VIZIER_WHO_SURVIVED_HIS_OFFICE.md`

**Finding:** **OVERLAP — CONSOLIDATE WITH GENUINE EXTENSION.**

Already established:
- Returning Predecessor challenge;
- office separate from holder;
- formal authority distinct from effective influence;
- actor-specific recognition of authority.

New extension:
- formal succession can complete before the surrounding trust/access/habit network reorganizes;
- predecessor non-action may be causally necessary for successor authority to become socially real;
- informal access can create a shadow channel without legal usurpation.

**Primary home:** Institutional Dynamics — Continuity/succession + Offices/roles + Effective influence.

The existing Returning Predecessor challenge should point here rather than spawning another challenge family.

### 4.4 Residual legitimacy versus decaying competence

**Sources:** both retirement tablets

**Finding:** **LATER NEW REFINEMENT.**

Existing program separates authority, capability, recognition and influence.

New distinction:
- prestige/recognition may remain high while current operational knowledge decays.

This should be preserved as a temporal relation among:
- former authority;
- current competence;
- effective influence;
- recognition/prestige.

Do not encode “elder wisdom” as a trait.

### 4.5 Structural failure under sincere/obedient actors

**Source:** `THE_PALACE_OF_OBEDIENT_SCRIBES.md`

**Finding:** **OVERLAP — CONSOLIDATE.**

Already represented:
- Work, Knowledge and Coordination;
- coordination/supervision cost;
- integration bottlenecks;
- hidden prerequisites;
- Two-Ledger Integrator;
- project assurance lessons about common-mode blind spots.

New emphasis:
- serious institutional error need not require corruption, ambition or disobedience;
- decomposition/common abstractions can create correlated blindness;
- human informality/disobedience can sometimes repair as well as damage formal systems.

**Primary home:** Institutional Dynamics J — Tasks, responsibility, skill and knowledge.

**Secondary status:** project/governance analogy.

Do not create a standalone “obedient scribes” subsystem.

### 4.6 Authority/record recognition is not truth

**Source:** `THE_ARCHIVE_CORRECTED_ITS_KEEPER.md`

**Finding:** **REDUNDANT CORE.**

Already established:
- objective history versus official/remembered history;
- durable artifact does not imply true artifact;
- recognized claimant does not imply objective legitimacy;
- engine truth is separate from actor/institutional recognition.

No new concept family required for that base distinction.

### 4.7 Good-faith false records, correction history, and corrigibility

**Source:** `THE_ARCHIVE_CORRECTED_ITS_KEEPER.md`

**Finding:** **LATER NEW / EXTENSION.**

New payload beyond the established truth/record distinction:
- false institutional records can arise honestly;
- correction is itself a historical event;
- silent replacement erases causally real institutional error;
- preservation is a chain of capture/transfer/storage/retrieval/interpretability rather than a Boolean;
- reliable institutions need checkability, not merely trustworthy keepers.

**Primary home:** Institutional Dynamics H — Artifacts and documentary memory, plus L — Multiple genealogies.

**Cross-cutting extension:** Institutional corrigibility.

### 4.8 Sanctioned opposition / credible contradiction

**Source:** `THE_PRIEST_PAID_TO_DOUBT.md`

**Finding:** **LATER NEW, WITH PROJECT/GOVERNANCE ANALOGY.**

The current project already uses this pattern procedurally; that project-process observation is not itself a simulation requirement.

Genuinely reusable future questions:
- how contradiction remains socially viable;
- how a critic gains trust by demonstrated willingness to impose real cost;
- how adversarial roles can hold strong negative authority without acquiring broad positive sovereignty;
- how criticism becomes theater if incentives/access are controlled by the reviewed office;
- how professional opposition can itself become distorted.

**Primary home:** Institutional Dynamics I — Procedures/practices + C/D — roles/authority.

**Current status:** future institutional question, not SFL v0.

### 4.9 “Criticism requires a good ruler”

**Source:** *The Priest Paid to Doubt*

**Finding:** **PROJECT/GOVERNANCE ANALOGY FIRST; FUTURE SOCIAL QUESTION SECOND.**

The project has direct evidence that leadership behavior affects whether independent criticism is usable.

As simulation theory, this remains a broader question about how surrounding incentive/relationship structures make formal procedures real or ceremonial.

Preserve as a challenge, not as a current mechanic.

## 5. True redundancy summary

These ideas do **not** need new future concept families because the project already preserves them durably:

- office/role is distinct from holder;
- formal authority is distinct from effective influence;
- recognition is distinct from objective truth;
- official/remembered history may differ from causal history;
- durable artifacts may be false;
- higher-order group descriptions must remain grounded in individuals;
- actor-specific knowledge must not globally synchronize;
- effective centrality should not default to one “importance” scalar;
- institutional identity/capability/authority/recognition are separable.

The new tablets remain valuable as source cases and refinements, but rediscovering these base distinctions should point to the existing conceptual home rather than create parallel theory.

## 7. Resulting conceptual architecture

The smallest adequate organization is now:

### Social Fabric / SFL

Current accepted substrate.

### Social Epistemic Dynamics

Broad lower-level future program for:
- mediated relationship ancestry;
- group information environments without group minds;
- distributed social representation/reputation;
- informational brokerage as a network phenomenon;
- plural network memory.

### Institutional Dynamics

Higher-order future program for:
- identity/continuity;
- offices/authority;
- capability/participation;
- artifacts/memory;
- tasks/coordination;
- effective influence;
- genealogy;
- post-office succession;
- institutional corrigibility;
- role-conditioned institutional effects where relevant.

### Richer Individual Agency / Attention

Not yet a full program, but a clearly preserved future seam for:
- role-conditioned salience;
- richer motivation;
- attention/capacity;
- planning.

Do not create a full new program until more evidence requires it.

## 8. Slice-2 consequence

The tablet systematization is complete enough that Slice 2 may proceed without conceptual debt.

The only current-slice effects are **interpretive/verification lenses**, not new semantics:

- keep actor subjective state sparse and route/provenance-aware;
- test real divergence across actors;
- preserve stale/contested states;
- keep direct and communicated evidence distinguishable;
- preserve anti-omniscience;
- make subjective state causally visible in action availability;
- do not smuggle future trust/reputation/brokerage/group-mind mechanisms into the slice.

Everything else in the tablets is now durably assigned to a later conceptual home or identified as already-established.
