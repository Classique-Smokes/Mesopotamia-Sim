## Disposition vocabulary

- **DERIVED HOME** — a current non-authoritative synthesis/navigation artifact where later retrieval should normally begin.
- **FULLY ABSORBED** — the source remains preserved, while its reusable conceptual payload has a durable derived home and cross-links.
- **CONTEXT-ONLY / METHODOLOGY** — useful provenance, explanation, observer method, transfer context, or project/governance analogy; no independent concept family is required.
- **REDUNDANT DERIVATIVE** — useful decomposition, transcription, combined reading copy, or analysis, but not independent evidence or a distinct conceptual payload after reconciliation.
- **SUPERSEDED SOURCE LINEAGE** — retained for provenance; a later source version supersedes it for ordinary reading, while omitted material has been checked for unique conceptual delta.
- **UNRESOLVED** — a bounded uncertainty remains and is recorded explicitly rather than silently filled in.

## Whole-library conceptual integration

The smallest adequate integrated structure is:

1. **SFL / Social Fabric** — accepted lower-level substrate where already authoritative; conceptual material does not amend it.
2. **Social Epistemic Dynamics** — lower-level social topology ↔ information-route ↔ actor-held representation loop.
3. **Institutional Dynamics** — higher-order identity, continuity, offices, authority, capability, recognition, artifacts, procedures, coordination, influence, genealogy, institutional age.
