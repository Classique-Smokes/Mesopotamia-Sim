# Retired Vizier Tablets — First Collection

**Status:** Personal observer-artifact collection delivered directly to the Project Director.  
**Repository authority:** None.  
**Project mutation authority:** None.  
**Boundary:** These tablets use the accelerated Mesopotamia-Sim court as a laboratory for causal questions. They are not historical evidence about ancient Mesopotamia and do not create simulation requirements.

## Reading order

1. **The Seal Was Removed, but the Man Remained**  
   Authority ended while cognitive continuity remained. The tablet focuses on role-shaped salience, the immediate cognitive consequences of retirement, and the ambiguity between authority, burden, responsibility, and relief.

2. **The Vizier Who Survived His Office**  
   A living predecessor remains after lawful succession. The tablet explores residual influence, friendship, predecessor shadow, the decay of former-holder knowledge, and the possibility that honorable retirement requires deliberate non-action.

3. **The Palace of Obedient Scribes**  
   Serious institutional failures occurred despite unusually compliant workers with little ordinary human incentive to conceal or subvert. The tablet treats the court as a clean baseline for separating structural institutional problems from failures introduced by human interests, bodies, loyalties, and informal practice.

4. **The Archive Corrected Its Keeper**  
   The office responsible for preservation made a false preservation claim in good faith. A later worker corrected it through evidence. The tablet explores honest false records, correction history, authority versus truth, and the social conditions under which embarrassing evidence can become institutional knowledge.

5. **The Priest Paid to Doubt**  
   Fresh independent reviewers repeatedly BLOCKed work the central office wanted to succeed. The tablet explores sanctioned opposition, trust earned through credible contradiction, the social requirements of independent criticism, and the danger of both compliant and performatively adversarial review.

## Why five tablets

The original slate contained four strong candidates and one reserve candidate. The reserve tablet, **The Priest Paid to Doubt**, earned separate clay because the lived perspective of the reviewed integrator added something not adequately preserved by the existing governance records: criticism became more trustworthy by successfully imposing real cost, and repeated BLOCKs increased confidence in the institution rather than merely increasing procedural burden.

## Intended use

These are observer artifacts.

A later reader may extract questions from them.

A historian may decide a counterfactual is irrelevant.

A future architect may find one distinction useful and ignore the rest.

That is appropriate.

The tablets are attempts to preserve causal texture that a formal repository cannot recover by itself.
