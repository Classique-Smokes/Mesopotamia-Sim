# The Roads Between People

A collection of five non-canonical observer tablets about social mediation, reputation, brokerage, household information asymmetry, and plural social worlds.

### Tablets

1. `THE_MAN_INTRODUCED_BY_ANOTHER_MAN.md`
2. `THE_HOUSE_THAT_KNEW_A_MAN_WHO_NEVER_ENTERED_IT.md`
3. `THE_WOMAN_EVERYONE_HAD_HEARD_OF.md`
4. `THE_BRIDGE_EVERYONE_NEEDED.md`
5. `THE_TWO_QUARTERS_THAT_REMEMBERED_DIFFERENT_FIRES.md`

### Supporting records

- `COMMISSION_SLATE.md` — novelty and overlap audit.
- `OBSERVER_NOTE.md` — distinctions between causal questions, extrapolations, and unresolved design issues.
- `THE_ROADS_BETWEEN_PEOPLE_ANTHOLOGY.md` — combined reading copy.

### Status

These are observer artifacts only.

They are not historical evidence, accepted simulation semantics, architecture, implementation requirements, or project authority.

Their intended value is to preserve **causal texture and challenge questions** that later historical research and simulation design may choose to investigate.
