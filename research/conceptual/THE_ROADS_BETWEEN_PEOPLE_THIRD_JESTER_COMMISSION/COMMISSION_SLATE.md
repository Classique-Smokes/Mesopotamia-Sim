# Commission Slate — The Roads Between People
## Novelty / overlap audit for the third Jester commission

**Status:** Planning record for non-canonical observer artifacts  
**Purpose:** Show why each tablet earns separate clay and which previously documented themes it deliberately avoids duplicating.

---

## Collection question

> **How do social relationships mediate what people know about other people, and how do those mediated representations then reshape the social network that carried them?**

The collection deliberately treats palace authority as one special case rather than the foundation.

The progression is:

**triad → household → reputation network → broker position → larger plural social worlds**

---

## Tablet 1 — The Man Introduced by Another Man

**Concrete case:** Two people have no direct tie. A mutual acquaintance gives each a model of the other before their first meeting.

**New causal payload:** A third party can become causally embedded in the later history of a dyadic relationship. Direct relationships can possess social prehistory outside the pair.

**Overlap deliberately avoided:**
- not the Foreign Observer case;
- not generic subjective recognition;
- not patronage;
- not office mediation.

**Ambiguity preserved:** Did the intermediary create the later relationship's shape, or merely accelerate dispositions that would have emerged anyway?

---

## Tablet 2 — The House That Knew a Man Who Never Entered It

**Concrete case:** One household member knows an outsider directly; the rest receive different versions through him and coordinate behavior without sharing one belief.

**New causal payload:** Dense small groups can multiply the reach of individual relationships. A household can become an information environment without becoming a collective mind.

**Overlap deliberately avoided:**
- not household identity or membership mechanics;
- not magical household knowledge;
- not “the household believes X” as primitive state.

**Ambiguity preserved:** When several household members act compatibly from different beliefs, when is higher-order shorthand legitimate?

---

## Tablet 3 — The Woman Everyone Had Heard Of

**Concrete case:** A woman's socially consequential image spreads beyond her acquaintance set through living retellings.

**New causal payload:** Reputation is distributed across other people's representations rather than existing as one global scalar. Social identity can travel farther than the person and feed back into later behavior.

**Overlap deliberately avoided:**
- unlike *Anonymous Artists*, the carrier is primarily living social transmission rather than artifacts;
- not generic “informal influence”;
- not a single reputation score.

**Ambiguity preserved:** Where does a person's socially effective identity end and other people's representations of her begin?

---

## Tablet 4 — The Bridge Everyone Needed

**Concrete case:** An ordinary person connects otherwise costly-to-connect social clusters. Repeated mediation creates dependency, discretion, burden, and eventual power.

**New causal payload:** Information brokerage can create network centrality before office, status, or wealth formalizes it.

**Overlap deliberately avoided:**
- unlike *Two Ledgers*, centrality comes from mediating social worlds rather than integrating assigned tasks;
- unlike the Master Architect office, the role begins without formal jurisdiction;
- unlike the king, authority is not the source of brokerage.

**Ambiguity preserved:** At what point does useful brokerage become power, and must the broker recognize the transition?

---

## Tablet 5 — The Two Quarters That Remembered Different Fires

**Concrete case:** One event is witnessed partially and transmitted through two clustered social networks. Different local histories become self-reinforcing through later action.

**New causal payload:** Plural social realities can emerge from ordinary network structure, trust, compression, and path dependence without requiring a central liar, centralized propaganda, or a collective mind.

**Overlap deliberately avoided:**
- not official-vs-causal genealogy alone;
- not the False Founder;
- not merely rumor;
- not “different actors know different things” in isolation.

**Ambiguity preserved:** When actors respond rationally to unequal evidence, what exactly does it mean to call one social world wrong?

---

## Exclusion rules used while drafting

The collection rejected or minimized any passage that would imply:

- household members automatically share knowledge;
- reputation is one global number;
- intermediaries transmit propositions without interpretation;
- trust is universal rather than relationship-specific;
- direct observation automatically erases prior mediated belief;
- false information requires malicious lying;
- authority is necessary for brokerage;
- the most informed person is necessarily the most influential;
- everyone eventually converges on objective truth;
- one communication graph substitutes for the social relations that create and weight communication.

---

## Hidden causal spine used for every tablet

Each story was required to support a chain of the form:

**initial relations → information event → transmission route → selection/transformation → recipient interpretation → social action → changed relations → changed future transmission**

The final tablets do not expose this as a mechanical schema because the purpose of the collection is to preserve causal texture, not to pre-design a simulation system.
