# Historical Evidence Register

**Purpose:** Preserve historical claims, sources, interpretation, uncertainty, context, and simulation relevance without turning research directly into mechanics.

## Core rule

**Evidence is not a mechanic.** A design decision derived from evidence must be separately proposed and accepted under the appropriate authority.

## Confidence labels

Use qualitative confidence tied to the recorded evidence, e.g. `LOW / MODERATE / HIGH`, with an explanation. Confidence is not a claim of universal applicability.
