## Purpose

Determine what the new observer stories add to the project's **conceptual question structure**, especially as SFL v0 moves into subjective knowledge/recognition, without promoting narrative observations into current simulation semantics.

The collections are vaguely related to earlier material but must not be presumed to form one ontology or one dependency chain.

## Retrieval for synthesis

Retrieve together:

1. both new source collections and their ingestion records;
2. `research/conceptual/THE_URUK_ACCIDENT/`;
3. `research/conceptual/INSTITUTIONAL_DYNAMICS/`;
4. `research/conceptual/PROPOSED_POST_V0_CONCEPTUAL_DIRECTIONS.md`;
5. Birth Run 001 reconciliation;
6. current Slice-2 discovery returns when available;
7. accepted SFL semantics/ADRs only when deciding whether a conceptual distinction is already represented, deferred, or outside v0.

## Required analysis

For every distinct causal theme extracted from the tablets:

- identify exact source tablet(s);
- distinguish direct source observation from Master Architect inference;
- identify overlap with an existing conceptual theme;
- identify genuinely new distinction/question if any;
- identify whether the question is:
  - already represented by accepted SFL machinery;
  - naturally observable in Slice 2;
  - naturally deferred to Slices 3–5;
  - post-v0/future research;
  - historical-research question;
  - project/governance analogy only;
- preserve negative boundaries and ambiguities stated by the source;
- resist collapsing multiple kinds of relation, authority, reputation, knowledge, memory, or institutional persistence into one universal abstraction.

## Output decision

After reading the complete corpora, choose the smallest adequate derived structure:

1. amend an existing Institutional Dynamics document if the material genuinely extends an already-defined seam;
2. create a sibling conceptual synthesis if the new material forms a distinct problem family;
3. add bounded challenge cases/questions to existing indexes;
4. preserve a tablet as source-only if no useful systematization is justified yet.

Do **not** decide the output structure in advance simply because the files arrived together.

## Current-slice boundary

The Roads collection is obviously adjacent to Slice-2 questions about mediated knowledge and recognition, but adjacency is not authority.

The Retired Vizier collection contains themes spanning role-shaped cognition, succession, institutional correction, and sanctioned opposition; much of it may belong far beyond Slice 2 or remain project/governance analogy only.

No delegated Slice-2 discovery worker may mine either collection for requirements.

Only after both the ground survey and Master Architect tablet synthesis return should any conceptual observation be considered for a consequential design/research promotion path.

## Completion condition

Systematization is complete when:

- source-to-derived provenance is explicit;
- old and new conceptual distinctions are reconciled without source rewriting;
- current-slice relevance is separated from future ambition;
- no story has been silently promoted into mechanics;
- the Project Director can inspect the derived map and see which ideas are old, new, overlapping, deferred, or merely evocative.
