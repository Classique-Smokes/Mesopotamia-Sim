# Initial Development Roadmap — Working Proposal

**Status:** WORKING / NON-AUTHORITATIVE  
**Foundation:** DEC-0001 ACCEPTED  
**Purpose:** Sequence the next work without prematurely turning simulation intent into programming architecture.

## Foundation objective

Build the smallest meaningful **Social Fabric Laboratory** capable of testing whether persistent household-scale organization can arise, persist, transform, divide, consolidate, and dissolve through the histories and interactions of individuals, relationships, obligations, resources/claims, and places.

The first implementation is a headless simulation laboratory, not a miniature final game.

Current scope deliberately does **not** require full food/subsistence, ageing, reproduction, demographic, production, or similar upstream engines. Where needed for testing the social fabric, controlled scenario events/fixtures may provide inputs such as death, entry, resource pressure, or relationship change without pretending those fixtures are final mechanics.

## Gate 0 — Evidence normalization — PARALLEL / NON-BLOCKING

Delegate RES-0001 proposition deduplication and reconciliation. This prepares clean historical evidence candidates and preserves disputes. It does not choose mechanics or architecture.

## Gate 1 — Foundational causal model — COMPLETE

`DEC-0001 — Foundational Social-Fabric Model` accepted on 2026-09-19.

## Organizational gate — Master Architect operating guide — CURRENT

Review and explicitly approve/revise/reject the proposed constitutional Master Architect Guide before the next major delegation wave. This establishes delegation context modes, bias controls, canonical compression, and project-folder hygiene for future agents.

## Gate 2 — Open technical architecture reconnaissance

After the operating guide is accepted, commission **problem-first** technical research around the accepted Social Fabric Laboratory objective.

The initial briefs should provide the accepted semantics, required observability/legibility, near-term proof objective, relevant scale horizon, and important exclusions—but should **not preselect software patterns, languages, engines, storage models, algorithms, libraries, or architecture families**.

Use the minimum framing needed for agents to understand the narrow technical problem. Where useful, dispatch multiple independent agents from the same neutral brief so the solution space emerges before the Master Architect creates a shortlist.

Specialist follow-up research may then probe or benchmark candidate approaches that actually emerged from reconnaissance.

## Gate 3 — Architectural synthesis and decisions

The Master Architect reconciles technical returns against DEC-0001, the Seed, evidence constraints, and project goals.

Consequential choices become explicit ADR/decision proposals with alternatives, recommendation, tradeoffs, scale/fidelity implications, and reversibility. Human approval is required at the appropriate level.

No foundational implementation begins merely because an auxiliary agent strongly recommended a stack or architecture.

## Gate 4 — Foundation subsystem specification

After the necessary architecture is accepted, write the first implementation-facing specification for the minimum Social Fabric Laboratory.

It should define only the accepted behavior, invariants, interfaces, observability requirements, assumptions, and exclusions required for the first proof.

## Gate 5 — Headless Social Fabric Laboratory

Implement a bounded synthetic social world optimized first for inspection, causal explanation, reproducibility, and experimentation.

The first proof is successful when lower-level social processes can generate and explain household-scale continuity and change without scripting household histories as fixed transitions.

The laboratory should be able to test conceptual cases including:

- continuity through turnover;
- partition without automatic social fission;
- later consolidation without scripted merger;
- support/obligation without forced co-residence;
- residence without automatic ownership;
- multidimensional social position;
- traceable lower-level causes for collective change.

These remain conceptual proof obligations until translated into accepted specifications.

## Gate 6 — Historical/mechanistic evaluation

Compare prototype behavior against reconciled RES-0001 mechanism evidence. Initially evaluate whether documented **kinds of processes** are representable coherently, not whether unsupported historical frequencies have been reproduced.

Material temporary calibration assumptions enter the Assumptions Register.

## Gate 7 — First upward extension

Only after the household/social-fabric foundation behaves coherently should the project select the next research/design layer. No temple, palace, city, economy, or other higher subsystem is assumed to be next until its need is decision-relevant.
