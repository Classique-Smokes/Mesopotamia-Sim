## 3. Fixture and producer authority

A witness may use declared lower-level fixture setup, but fixtures may not write endogenous results that the witness is intended to prove.

A witness beginning from an existing H may reuse only a state produced through a previously verified scenario whose producer identity, acceptance result, fixture audit, semantic/configuration version, and output identity are recorded. A fresh fixture may not write a Household ID, warrant, participation result, lifecycle result, or final Recognition state and call it verified.

Formation setup may seed declared people, grain, residence, kinship/marriage/attitude, lower-level subjective evidence not under acquisition test, and scheduled lower-level proposals/communications. It must not write Household, FormationWarrant, SustainingParticipant, CandidateRecognition when its derivation is under test, final H Recognition, lifecycle, head/office state, or lineage.

Participation/continuity setup may begin from a verified produced H and declared newcomer lower-level facts/evidence. It must not write ParticipationWarrant, ParticipationEndWarrant, SustainingParticipant addition/removal, ContinuationWarrant, lifecycle verdict, or final newcomer H Recognition.

For Household Recognition, actor-held evidence may be controlled only where acquisition itself is not the assertion target and provenance remains fixture-marked. No fixture may write recipient/final Recognition, treat engine-side H state as actor evidence, or invent generic observation entitlement.

Lineage setup may reuse verified predecessor histories and ordinary lower-level successor setup. It must not write LineageWarrant, DerivedFrom, predecessor classification, fresh-evidence verdict, or inherited causal state.

## 4. Oracle independence

Acceptance logic derives expected results from accepted public history/state rather than asking production classifiers/helpers for their own answer.

- formation: independently recompute lower-level predicate and founder evidence; do not use production formation/CandidateRecognition helpers as sole oracle;
- participation: independently check ParticipationWarrant prerequisites;
- continuity: follow direct warrant/history references, never endpoint similarity;
- lifecycle: reconstruct sustaining associations then apply 2+ / 1 / 0 only at stable closure;
- Recognition: use actor-specific state/provenance and paired runs; do not use production Recognition updater/conflict resolver as expected oracle;
- lineage: independently check successor formation, explicit predecessor exit sources, lifecycle timing, fresh-support ordering, cross-predecessor condition and ambiguity; do not use production lineage classifier;
- DerivedFrom / AncestorOf: rebuild independently from LineageWarrant;
- provision: independently calculate capacity from valid commitments, grain reserve and NeedsGrain;
- derived/cache state: prove structural absence as behavioral authority or discard/rebuild plus stale-cache challenge.
