# DEC-0005 — Adopt the Architecture Development Policy

**Status:** PROPOSED  
**Authority level:** Architectural process governance  
**Opened:** 2026-09-19

## Problem

The project now has enough technical research to make sophisticated implementation choices, but premature adoption of individually attractive technologies could produce unnecessary complexity, semantic drift, or a poor fit for the project's AI-assisted development conditions.

Future architecture decisions need a compact, durable rule set for progressive optimization and evidence-driven specialization.

## Proposal

Adopt `governance/ARCHITECTURE_DEVELOPMENT_POLICY.md` as the governing policy for future software-architecture selection and evolution.

## Consequence

Future architectural work must preserve semantic authority, favor measured progressive specialization, keep performance structures replaceable where practical, and make sophisticated infrastructure earn its complexity through evidence.

## Does not decide

No implementation stack, language, runtime, scheduler, database, concurrency model, persistence system, cognition framework, or optimization technology is selected.

## Evidence

- TRES-0002 Stage-I independent architecture-horizon research
- `research/technical/TRES-0002/STAGE_I_SYNTHESIS.md`
- Existing project governance and accepted ADRs
