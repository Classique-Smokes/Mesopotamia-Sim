## Problem / question

Should the accepted Master Architect operating doctrine be reorganized from one increasingly large always-loaded Guide into a smaller constitutional core with explicit routing to single-owner work-mode protocols, templates/checklists, and mechanical enforcement where mature?

## Proposed decision

Adopt the evaluated GOV-0001 candidate as the constitutional Master Architect rulebook architecture.

Preserve DEC-0002's role/authority intent while replacing the current monolithic operating-guide organization with:

**small constitutional core → triggered specialized protocol → task-instance template/checklist → mechanical enforcement where mature**

## Proposed binding consequences

If accepted:

1. `governance/MASTER_ARCHITECT_GUIDE.md` remains constitutional but becomes the compact evaluated core.
2. Detailed recurring delegation/research procedure is single-owned by `AGENT_DELEGATION_AND_RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md`.
3. Consequential specification→implementation handoff procedure is single-owned by `SPECIFICATION_TO_IMPLEMENTATION_ASSURANCE_PROTOCOL.md`.
4. Existing interpretation, promotion, architecture-development, escalation, and drift protocols remain their respective procedural owners.
5. The core may repeat short universal invariants/triggers, but detailed recurring procedure should have one primary owner.
6. Protocols are consequence-triggered; their existence does not make them mandatory for routine local work.
7. TRES-0010 durable lessons are preserved at protocol/template/checklist layers rather than appended wholesale to the always-loaded core.
8. Future recurring AI failures should continue moving toward appropriate environmental/mechanical enforcement when mature.
9. Fresh-agent discoverability from the repository entry path remains a completion requirement for governance promotion.
