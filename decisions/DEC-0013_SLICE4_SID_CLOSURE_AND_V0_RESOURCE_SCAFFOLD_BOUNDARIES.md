# DEC-0013 — Slice-4 SID Closure and V0 Resource-Scaffold Boundaries

**Status:** ACCEPTED  
**Authority:** Architectural / social-semantic  
**Opened:** 2026-09-24  
**Resolved:** 2026-09-24  
**Accepted by:** Project Director  
**Scope:** bounded SFL v0 Slice 4 SID closure plus interpretation/reversibility boundaries for the provisional v0 Household resource scaffold

## Problem

The first SID-ASSURANCE-v1 pilot over Slice 4 completed with six consequential semantic decision clusters still open:

1. Household material provision versus RepayDebt priority;
2. same-snapshot bridge entry versus accepted head succession before the same exit;
3. current head as eligible Household-support recipient;
4. current head creating/reconsidering their own persistent provision commitment;
5. self-nomination for Household head;
6. role appointment while Household is Inactive.

The Project Director intentionally held these choices behind TRES-0011 because the current Household resource/provision mechanism is deliberately crude and may later be substantially redesigned.

TRES-0011 is now complete. Its independent laboratory-fitness, architecture/reversibility, simulation-methodology and historical-pressure returns agree that the present scaffold can continue to serve SFL v0's bounded laboratory purpose, provided its mechanism-specific rules are prevented from hardening into a mature economic ontology or historical claim.

## Proposed decision

The Project Director explicitly approved this package. Adopt the following six bounded Slice-4 semantics.

### D1 — bounded Household material priority versus RepayDebt

For the current v0 same-cycle conflict over shared personal grain:

`eligible NeedsGrain Household support > other valid Household provision expenditure > RepayDebt`

RepayDebt remains a distinct debt action and retains its existing debt-claim/due-cycle social consequences.

This ordering is a v0 conflict-resolution rule only. It is not a historical, moral, legal or future-economic priority claim about Household obligations versus debt.

### D2 — snapshot-bound consent cohort for accepted appointment/succession

When an appointment/succession is validly proposed and accepted from a common committed decision snapshot:

- the required unanimity cohort is the set of current sustaining participants at that decision snapshot;
- every captured member must still satisfy relevant live eligibility/revalidation at commit;
- a person who becomes a sustaining participant later in the same cycle does not retroactively enter the already-accepted consent cohort;
- later entry does not confer head Recognition except through ordinary information/recognition semantics.

### D3 — current head may be eligible Household-support recipient

The current head remains in the ordinary eligible-needy sustaining-participant recipient class.

When head P is the recipient:

- use the proactive Household-support topology;
- do not create a P->P support request/response context;
- role authority and beneficiary identity remain distinct semantic capacities;
- ordinary current v0 funding/eligibility rules apply;
- P being NeedsGrain exposes neither private-X capacity nor standing commitment capacity under the current scaffold;
- Household support remains Household-originated and cannot become formation/continuity/lineage evidence.

This establishes only a v0 alias/eligibility case, not a historical entitlement or typical conflict-of-interest norm.

### D4 — current head may explicitly create/reconsider own persistent provision commitment in v0

A current head P who is otherwise eligible under the v0 scaffold may separately and explicitly authorize the same persistent standing-resource-access relation available to other contributors.

- office occupancy alone never creates the commitment or exposes private grain;
- P's private-resource authorization remains semantically distinct from role authority;
- no P->P ResponseDecisionContext or artificial self-negotiation is required;
- no second personal initiative is consumed merely to represent the second semantic capacity;
- ordinary reconsideration/cooldown/history rules apply to the commitment process;
- the exact route remains part of the current synthetic v0 HouseholdProvisionCommitment scaffold.

This is not a claim that Mesopotamian Household resources generally derived from individually owned property voluntarily committed to a Household, nor a commitment that the mature Household economy will retain HouseholdProvisionCommitment at all.

### D5 — self-nomination allowed through one explicit multi-capacity act

A current sustaining participant may nominate themself for a vacant head role.

The explicit self-nomination act must omission-detectably carry:

1. nomination;
2. nominee willingness/acceptance;
3. that same person's participant acceptance of the appointment.

Other current sustaining participants must still separately satisfy their required acceptance/recognition predicates.

No self-directed nomination response context is opened.

For nomination of another person, nomination still does not replace the nominator's separate participant-acceptance predicate.

### D6 — Inactive H may fill/hold an inert head role

Conditional on D5, an Inactive Household with exactly one sustaining participant may fill a vacant head role through the valid self-nomination/acceptance path.

- role occupancy may persist while H remains Inactive;
- H receives no HouseholdDecisionContext and no Household-mode initiative while Inactive;
- filling the role does not reactivate H;
- reactivation preserves the occupied persistent role;
- nontrivial succession to a different current participant is impossible while H is Inactive because only one current sustaining participant exists.

## V0 resource-scaffold boundary

The current resource subsystem is explicitly a **synthetic v0 material-grounding scaffold**.

The following are current laboratory mechanisms, not mature economic/historical commitments:

- personal grain as the sole material stock;
- reserve 2 / NeedsGrain exposure;
- HouseholdProvisionCommitment;
- commitment-only MobilizableCapacity;
- no Household treasury/pool;
- fixed-rank allocation;
- current Household material conflict priorities;
- transaction-specific current-head private contribution X.

The durable semantics to preserve through later redesign are narrower:

- collective material action must remain causally grounded;
- institutional authority and resource/property/obligation authority remain distinguishable;
- same-person multi-capacity acts preserve each required semantic predicate without fake self-negotiation;
- live material participation/preconditions revalidate before commit;
- successful effects preserve structured causal/funding provenance;
- standing derived capacity is not an independently authoritative Household wealth balance;
- resource mechanics do not define Household identity, participation, role identity/occupancy or recognition identity.

## Implementation / architecture guardrails

Before Slice-4 material implementation is accepted:

1. one authoritative live funding-resolution point must own the current funding algorithm;
2. semantic history must preserve explicit typed funding/source/authority provenance;
3. resolver dependency discovery must consume declared/evaluated material participation rather than duplicate funding policy;
4. MobilizableCapacity must remain rebuildable/non-authoritative derived state;
5. no generic asset/ownership/ledger framework is authorized merely to anticipate a future economy.

## Observation / scientific guardrail

The provisional resource scaffold is a declared experimental factor.

Before later semantic hardening is justified by observed social dynamics rather than direct accepted authority/local correctness, the project must test relevant observations across bounded resource-parameter variation, timing variation, at least one structurally different simple resource-input mechanism, and a small interaction design over the strongest factors.

Observation reports must distinguish claims that are only `within current v0 scaffold` from claims robust across tested variants.

A future resource/economic redesign requires rerunning materially dependent social observations.

## Future redesign trigger

Reopen Household resource semantics before accepting any later layer that requires general answers about Household assets, ownership/control, pooled stores, production, inheritance, taxes/dues, labor/service obligations, budgets/accounting, differentiated goods, dependent consumption claims, unequal asset rights, external institutional transfers, or historically calibrated economic behavior.

Later historical/economic research must select explicit period/place/status targets and investigate concrete bundles of possession/use/management/benefit/alienation/pledge/inheritance/obligation/authority rather than treating “ownership” as a single universal variable.

## Explicit nonclaims

This proposed decision does not:

- design the final Household economy;
- validate the current resource scaffold historically;
- establish a universal debt-versus-Household priority;
- establish historical household-head sovereignty;
- establish historical self-nomination/appointment practice;
- generalize HouseholdProvisionCommitment beyond v0;
- add generic asset, property-law, budget, ledger or settlement architecture;
- authorize implementation while the active Slice-4 block remains open;
- approve or dispatch the inherited-verification adaptation review unchanged.
