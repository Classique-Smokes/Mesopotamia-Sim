# DEC-0001 — Foundational Social-Fabric Model

**Status:** ACCEPTED  
**Authority level:** Constitutional  
**Accepted:** 2026-09-19  
**Approver:** Human Project Director

## Decision

The simulation begins from a **social fabric** composed of persistent individuals, material resources/places, historically meaningful relationships/claims/obligations, and events/processes that change them.

Households are **not primitive containers**. They emerge from durable configurations of lower-level social reality and may then acquire persistent identity, history, coordinated behavior, and causal influence of their own. Their continued state and action must remain explainable through the people, relationships, resources, rules/norms, places, and prior events that sustain them.

No single household association automatically implies kinship, co-residence, ownership, labor/service, support, dependency, institutional affiliation, or exclusive membership.

Later higher-order collectives may also become persistent causal actors, but must remain grounded in the lower-level social reality through which they exist and act. This does not imply that households, temples, palaces, cities, or other collectives share identical mechanics.

## Rationale

- The Project Seed establishes persistent individuals, fundamental relationships, households as the first major collective structure, emergence, recursive causality, and causal genealogy.
- RES-0001 showed that kinship, residence, property, support, labor, dependency, institutional connection, and spatial/documentary association must remain distinguishable rather than being inferred from one household-membership variable.
- This foundation allows later historical nuance to be expressed through interactions among general social processes rather than accumulated special-case scripts.

## Consequences

- Foundational design must preserve distinguishable relationships/claims and the histories that create, change, transmit, contest, fulfill, or terminate them.
- Resource/place identity must be separable from claims, use, occupation, or benefit.
- Household continuity must be capable of surviving turnover in particular people, assets, places, or relations.
- Causal observability and historical legibility are architectural concerns, not presentation-only features.

## Not decided

Programming language, engine, storage/data architecture, execution/time model, individual decision algorithm, household-recognition algorithm/thresholds, historical frequencies, demographic/lifecycle systems, performance strategy, UI/player role, and concrete representations of later institutions remain unresolved.
