# RES-0001 — Shared Research Context

**Operational orientation only.** The Project Seed remains authoritative.

## Accepted research frame

The Human Project Director explicitly approved Old Babylonian Nippur, approximately 1900–1600 BCE, emphasizing private/legal archives and archaeologically contextualized residential evidence, as the project's first bounded historical research anchor.

This is a choice of **where to begin research**, not a claim that Nippur is representative of Mesopotamia as a whole.

## Why this research exists

The project aims to build an autonomous, historically grounded simulation from persistent individuals → relationships → households → larger structures. The household is treated as broader than a modern nuclear family or mere co-residential unit: it may involve kin, dependents, workers, property, productive resources, obligations, authority, and accumulated history.

The research program therefore seeks historically specific evidence about how those lower-level social realities actually operated before the project decides how to represent them computationally.

## Founding safeguards researchers must preserve

1. **Context before generalization.** Evidence from one period, city, archive, text genre, excavation area, or social stratum must not silently become universal.
2. **Evidence versus interpretation.** Distinguish what a primary text or archaeological context directly supports from a scholar's reconstruction or theory.
3. **Uncertainty remains visible.** Preserve meaningful disagreement, lacunae, translation uncertainty, dating uncertainty, provenance problems, and sampling bias.
4. **Evidence is not mechanics.** Do not turn historical findings into simulation rules, variables, algorithms, or entity designs.
5. **Avoid modern defaults.** Do not assume modern nuclear-family, private-property, employment, state/firm/family, free/slave, or residence/membership categories map cleanly onto Old Babylonian Nippur.
6. **Preserve chronology.** “Old Babylonian Nippur” spans changing political and documentary circumstances. Use the most precise date/regnal context available.
7. **Preserve provenance.** Record city, excavation area/house/archive, tablet provenance, publication/corpus ID, and source genre when available.
8. **Negative evidence is weak by default.** Absence from a surviving archive or legal genre is not automatically evidence that a practice did not exist.

## Authority

The auxiliary researcher is advisory. It may challenge premises, identify contradictions, recommend further research, or say the evidence is inadequate. It may not modify canonical project state or declare historical findings to be accepted simulation design.
