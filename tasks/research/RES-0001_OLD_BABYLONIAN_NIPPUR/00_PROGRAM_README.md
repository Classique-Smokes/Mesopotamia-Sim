# RES-0001 — Old Babylonian Nippur Household Foundations Research Program

**Status:** RETURNS COMPLETE / RECONCILIATION PENDING  
**Commissioned:** 2026-09-19  
**Commissioned by:** Master Architect following explicit Human Project Director approval  
**Research frame:** Old Babylonian Nippur, approximately 1900–1600 BCE, emphasizing private/legal archives and archaeologically contextualized residential evidence.

## Purpose

This is the project's first substantive historical research program. It investigates the individual → relationship → household foundation before any simulation mechanics are proposed.

The frame is deliberately bounded. Findings from Old Babylonian Nippur are **not** automatically evidence for all Mesopotamia, all Old Babylonian cities, or all periods.

## Parent research question

> How were individuals organized into, related within, and differentiated inside households in Old Babylonian Nippur, and through what documented mechanisms did those households acquire resources, obligations, continuity, authority, and membership over time?
