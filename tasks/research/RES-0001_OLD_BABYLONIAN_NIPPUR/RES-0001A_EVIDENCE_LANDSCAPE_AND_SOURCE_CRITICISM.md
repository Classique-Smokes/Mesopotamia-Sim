# Research Task Packet — RES-0001A Evidence Landscape and Source Criticism

**Task ID:** RES-0001A  
**Commissioned by:** Master Architect  
**Status:** OPEN

## 1. Question

What is the actual evidentiary landscape for studying individuals, relationships, and households in Old Babylonian Nippur: which source types, archives, excavation contexts, and chronological clusters survive, what social populations do they disproportionately expose, and what claims can or cannot responsibly be made from them?

## 2. Why it matters now

All other RES-0001 workstreams depend on knowing what kind of evidence survives. This task protects the program from mistaking legal archives for a census, scribal exercises for observed transactions, or one excavated neighborhood/archive for the city as a whole.

## 3. Relevant project context

The approved research frame is Old Babylonian Nippur, approximately 1900–1600 BCE, with emphasis on private/legal archives and archaeologically contextualized residential evidence. The larger project builds historically from individuals → relationships → households, but this task does **not** design that model.

## 5. Specific research scope

Map, as far as the scholarship permits:

- the chronological distribution of the Old Babylonian Nippur evidence, including meaningful political/regnal phases rather than treating 1900–1600 BCE as uniform;
- major excavation areas, residential quarters, houses, archives, and find contexts relevant to private social/economic life;
- source genres: legal contracts, administrative records, letters, school/model contracts, lexical/literary material where socially relevant, and archaeological evidence;
- archival formation and survival: why documents were kept, where they were found, and whether collections are family/private, institutional, school-related, secondary deposits, or uncertain;
- publication/corpus pathways and important concordances;
- overrepresented and underrepresented social groups;
- what prosopography can reconstruct and where it becomes speculative;
- key methodological disputes or changes in interpretation in the scholarship.

Produce a **source map** other agents can use during reconciliation.

## 6. Boundaries / out of scope

Do not attempt to answer the whole household question substantively. Do not propose mechanics. Do not create a single “typical Nippur household.” Do not infer population frequencies from documentary frequencies without a defensible denominator.

## 7. Required output emphasis

In addition to the shared return format, include:

1. a chronology/context matrix;
2. a table of major archives/house contexts and their known biases;
3. a source-genre reliability/use matrix stating what each genre is strong and weak evidence for;
4. a list of the most consequential source-critical warnings the Master Architect must apply when reconciling the other packets.

## 8. Authority reminder

This task is advisory. You may identify evidence limits and challenge assumptions, but you may not create project decisions, simulation requirements, or mechanics.
