### B. Slice 3 / Slice 4 boundary

Resolve from accepted authority, not preference:

- whether Slice 3 requires provision commitment **state/capacity grounding only**, or actual household-originated spending;
- whether any provision action requires a head and therefore belongs to Slice 4;
- whether household recognition exists/continues independently of head recognition;
- which collective capability claim Slice 3 can truthfully demonstrate before a representative role exists.

If accepted sources do not uniquely determine the boundary, identify the exact decision needed. Do not choose it yourself.

4. if not, the smallest consequential semantic question that must be escalated.

Do not invent a general organization-equivalence theory.
