## 5. What counts as a candidate frontier surface

Identify a prior semantic surface when the new Slice-4 layer might plausibly:

- enable it;
- suppress/narrow it;
- add a gate/precondition;
- reroute its authority/resource/knowledge path;
- compete for the same resource/capacity;
- change its provenance/identity meaning;
- carry/terminate/transfer it across a new transition;
- alter whether it is reachable;
- create a meaningful same-PersonId alias with one of its positions.

Do not enumerate every existing action.

Focus on material causal adjacency.

## 6. Required method

For each candidate frontier surface:

1. name the new Slice-4 surface;
2. name the prior semantic surface/capability/path;
3. explain in 1–3 sentences why an interaction is plausible;
4. identify the interaction mechanism, such as:
   - read/write;
   - gate/precondition;
   - resource/capacity;
   - authority/control;
   - knowledge/Recognition;
   - lifecycle/transition;
   - topology/path;
   - participant alias;
   - causal/provenance retargeting;
5. state confidence that it belongs in the frontier:
   - HIGH;
   - MEDIUM;
   - LOW.

Do **not** classify the semantic outcome as allowed/prohibited/deferred/unresolved in Pass A.

Do **not** recommend a rule.
