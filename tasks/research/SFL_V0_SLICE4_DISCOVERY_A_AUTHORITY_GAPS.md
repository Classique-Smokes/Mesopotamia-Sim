- persistent `HouseholdHeadRole(H)`;

- unanimity of current SustainingParticipants;
- appointment;

- succession under the same role identity;

For each classify:

- REQUIRED in Slice 4;
- inherited/regression only;
- deferred to Slice 5;

- blocked by unresolved semantic gap.

### B. Office / holder / authority / Recognition boundary

From accepted authority, state explicitly which facts remain distinct:

- H exists;
- role R exists;
- P occupies R;
- P has accepted authority in a specific scope;
- actor X recognizes P as current holder;
- X may hold stale predecessor evidence;
- H has current material backing/capacity.

Identify any wording that could tempt implementation to collapse these into one boolean or one engine-global fact.

### C. Appointment / succession / vacancy boundary

Determine the exact accepted rules for:

- initial appointment into a vacant role;
- outgoing-head succession before vacating;
- role identity across occupant turnover;
- head exit without valid successor;
- nominee/current-participant eligibility;
- unanimity and nominee consent;
- direct Recognition acquisition for appointment/succession participants;
- outsider correction only through accepted evidence route.

If accepted sources do not determine an edge case, identify the smallest exact question instead of choosing a rule.

### D. Same-cycle authority/precondition races — LATER-04

Treat `LATER-04 — authority-destroying transition / authority-dependent action race` as a fired Slice-4 trigger.

Determine which races are already semantically determined and which, if any, require Director-level resolution. Inspect at minimum:

- head exits while H has accepted Household action;
- head succession versus accepted old-head-authorized action;
- nominee/participant eligibility changing in the same cycle;
- H becoming Inactive/Dissolved while a Household action is pending;
- provision capacity changing before authorized spend;
- head Recognition becoming stale/Contested before commit;
- mediated-marriage authority loss before commit.

Separate:

- general revalidation already fixed by SPEC §9.2;
- semantic priority explicitly accepted;
- unresolved symmetry that may use disclosed technical fallback;
- genuinely missing authority precedence.

Do not invent a general constitutional theory.
