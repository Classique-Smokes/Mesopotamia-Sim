**Status:** READY FOR PARALLEL DISPATCH

**Common baseline:** to be frozen as `tres-0011-v0-resource-scaffold-wave-v1`

## 1. Why this wave exists

The Slice-4 SID pilot has exposed and clarified several interactions around Household material action, persistent provision commitments, office-holder private supplementation, resource conflict, and dual-capacity office holders.

The Project Director's current concern is not primarily whether the six open Slice-4 choices are locally coherent.

The deeper concern is:

> **Are we making locally sensible choices on top of a deliberately crude Household-resource scaffold that will later be redesigned so heavily that v0 stops serving its intended role as a trustworthy laboratory?**

The Director expects future historical/economic research may substantially change:

- ownership/control distinctions;
- Household versus personal resources;
- contribution/obligation mechanisms;
- production/labor/land/material flows;
- pooling/accounting;
- institutional resource authority.

This wave tests whether that uncertainty is healthy and reversible, or whether it invalidates current v0 direction.

## 5. What this wave is NOT

This wave is not:

- a referendum on whether current resource semantics are historically true;
- a request to design the final Household economy;
- permission to add speculative abstractions;
- an instruction to reopen all of SFL v0;
- an implementation task;
- authority to promote the Director's current D1–D6 inclinations.

## 6. Questions the final MA synthesis must answer

After all four returns, the Master Architect must answer:

1. **Purpose:** Can v0 still truthfully call itself a social-mechanism laboratory with this resource scaffold?
2. **Claims:** What structural conclusions can v0 legitimately support, and what economic/historical conclusions must it not support?
3. **Reversibility:** Can the resource model be replaced later without rewriting Household identity, office, agency, history, and the reference kernel?
4. **Guardrails:** What is the smallest durable wording/test/seam needed now to prevent placeholder hardening?
5. **Timing:** Should the six Slice-4 decisions be promoted now, narrowed as explicitly v0-only, or held until deeper resource research?
6. **Trigger:** What evidence would force a resource redesign before later institutions build on this scaffold?
7. **Future research:** What questions belong in a later dedicated Household economy/resource research program?

## 7. Final synthesis verdict vocabulary

The Master Architect should ultimately choose one of:

- **PROCEED — V0 SCAFFOLD IS FIT AND REPLACEABLE**
- **PROCEED WITH EXPLICIT V0 / REDESIGN GUARDRAILS**
- **HOLD RESOURCE-COUPLED SLICE-4 SEMANTICS FOR RESEARCH**
- **REOPEN V0 RESOURCE FOUNDATION BEFORE CONTINUING**

The verdict must be evidence-based rather than majority vote across scribes.
