- create/edit files under `research/conceptual/` as explicitly permitted by their packet;
- update task-status/navigation files explicitly permitted by their packet;

Delegates may **not**:

- edit accepted SPEC/ADRs/decisions;
- create current implementation requirements;
- alter governance;

- treat observer tales as historical evidence;
- turn a vivid analogy directly into a mechanic;
- rewrite source artifacts merely to make them cleaner.

## Shared preservation rule

For each substantive idea, final bureaucracy must make one of these dispositions explicit:

- **ABSORBED — EXISTING HOME**;
- **ABSORBED — NEW EXTENSION OF EXISTING FAMILY**;
- **NEW FUTURE CONCEPT FAMILY / SEAM**;
- **CROSS-PROGRAM CHALLENGE CASE**;
- **PROJECT/GOVERNANCE ANALOGY ONLY**;
- **EXPLANATORY / COMMISSION / TRANSFER CONTEXT ONLY**;
- **REDUNDANT CORE — SOURCE RETAINED**;
- **SUPERSEDED VERSION — UNIQUE DELTA PRESERVED**;
- **UNRESOLVED / NEEDS FUTURE RESEARCH**.

No source may disappear merely because it is redundant; redundancy means its **idea** has a durable home and its source remains intact.
