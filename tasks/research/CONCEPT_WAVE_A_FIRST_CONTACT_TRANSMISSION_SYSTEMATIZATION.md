## Question

The project needs the causal/conceptual essence without turning either project's lore or workflow into simulation authority.

## Core analytical duties

1. Build a source census with path, source role, lineage/relationship, and whether it is primary response, translation, advisory transfer, retrospective narration, or explanation.
2. Extract **ideas**, not document summaries.
3. Separate:
   - cross-project cultural/institutional transmission;
   - translation and semantic preservation across local vocabularies;
   - selective adoption vs wholesale import;
   - native reinterpretation / retrospective continuity / founding narratives;
   - institutional self-description and reflexivity;
   - official/native history vs objective causal genealogy;
   - transfer artifacts becoming later evidence;
   - observer effects / contact altering the thing observed;
   - project-process lessons that are governance analogies only;
   - Wonder-Gather-specific technical advice with no general conceptual payload.
4. Reconcile each idea against current conceptual homes.
5. Do not create a new program merely because the first-contact story is vivid. Prefer an existing family unless there is a genuinely distinct causal question.
6. Preserve uncertainty and conflicting perspectives where source accounts disagree.
